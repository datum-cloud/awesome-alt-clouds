## What makes Formbricks different

Formbricks positions itself as the open-source alternative to proprietary experience management platforms like Qualtrics or SurveySparrow. Unlike traditional cloud providers that offer generic infrastructure, Formbricks provides a specialized vertical SaaS solution focused entirely on customer feedback and user experience data. Its core differentiator is the combination of an open-source codebase with a managed cloud offering, allowing organizations to maintain full data sovereignty.

The platform is built with a privacy-first architecture, ensuring compliance with GDPR and CCPA out of the box. It supports multiple survey deployment methods, including link-based surveys, website overlays, and deep integrations into web applications via React components. This flexibility allows developers to embed surveys directly into their product workflows without leaking user data to third-party analytics providers.

For teams requiring self-hosting, Formbricks offers a straightforward Docker-based deployment process. This appeals to security-conscious enterprises and developers who want to avoid vendor lock-in while still utilizing a modern, well-maintained stack. The platform’s focus on "Experience Data" rather than just raw survey responses allows for deeper insights into user sentiment across the entire customer journey.

## Pricing model

Formbricks operates on a freemium subscription model. The self-hosted version is completely free and open-source (MIT License), allowing unlimited users and surveys if you manage your own infrastructure.

The managed cloud version offers a free tier that includes basic survey features and a limited number of monthly responses. Paid plans scale based on the number of monthly responses and features such as advanced analytics, team collaboration, and SSO. Specific pricing tiers are available on their pricing page, but the model is generally transparent with no hidden usage fees beyond response limits. This stands out from typical cloud pricing by offering a truly free, unlimited option for those willing to self-host.

## When it fits

- **Privacy-Compliant Organizations:** Teams in the EU or regulated industries that cannot send user data to US-based third-party survey tools.
- **Developer-Led Product Teams:** Companies using React or similar frameworks that need to embed surveys natively into their applications.
- **Cost-Conscious Startups:** Organizations that want enterprise-grade survey capabilities but cannot justify the high cost of Qualtrics or Medallia.
- **Open-Source Advocates:** Teams that prefer to audit code and control their own data infrastructure.

## When it doesn't

- **Complex Market Research:** Heavy academic or large-scale market research requiring complex sampling and advanced statistical modeling tools found in enterprise suites.
- **Non-Technical Marketing Teams:** Users who prefer drag-and-drop builders with no technical setup or hosting requirements may find the self-hosting option daunting, though the cloud version mitigates this.

## Inclusion criteria

Formbricks meets all three inclusion criteria:

1.  **Transparent Pricing:** Pricing tiers are clearly listed on the website.
2.  **Self-Service Signup:** Users can sign up for the cloud version or access the code for self-hosting without sales intervention.
3.  **Public SLA/Status Page:** The provider offers an SLA for their cloud offering and maintains a status page for uptime monitoring.
