## What makes LaunchDarkly different

LaunchDarkly is not a traditional infrastructure cloud provider; it is a specialized SaaS platform for **feature management** and **runtime control**. While hyperscalers like AWS or GCP provide the underlying compute and storage, LaunchDarkly sits on top of your application stack to provide granular control over software behavior without redeployment. Its core differentiator is the ability to toggle features, target specific user segments, and manage AI agent behaviors in real-time.

The platform has evolved from simple feature flags to a comprehensive "Runtime Control" system, including **CodeControl** for code-level safety and **AgentControl** for managing autonomous AI agents. This allows engineering teams to reduce risk, accelerate release cycles, and maintain compliance in complex, AI-driven environments. It integrates with major CI/CD pipelines and cloud providers but does not offer infrastructure services itself.

## Pricing model

LaunchDarkly does not publish public pricing tiers on its website. As noted in the inclusion criteria, it requires a demo or sales contact to obtain pricing. Industry estimates and third-party reviews suggest pricing ranges from **$20,000 to over $120,000 per year**, heavily dependent on the number of team members, environments, and advanced features like AI Agent Control. This enterprise-focused, opaque pricing model contrasts sharply with the transparent, metered, or self-serve pricing of typical cloud infrastructure providers.

## When it fits

- **Enterprise Engineering Teams:** Organizations with large development teams needing robust collaboration, audit trails, and permission management for feature releases.
- **High-Risk Releases:** Companies requiring strict rollback capabilities and gradual rollout controls (canary releases, dark launching) to minimize production incidents.
- **AI-Driven Applications:** Teams building and deploying autonomous AI agents that require runtime safety controls, guardrails, and behavioral monitoring via AgentControl.
- **Compliance-Heavy Industries:** Financial or healthcare sectors where feature changes must be tracked, approved, and audited rigorously.

## When it doesn't

- **Infrastructure Needs:** This platform does not provide compute, storage, networking, or database services. It is an application-layer tool, not a cloud provider.
- **Small Teams/Startups:** The high cost and enterprise sales process make it prohibitive for small teams or early-stage startups with limited budgets.
