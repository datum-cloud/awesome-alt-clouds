## What makes Bolster different

Bolster does not provide compute, storage, or networking infrastructure like traditional cloud providers. Instead, it operates as a specialized Digital Risk Protection (DRP) platform focused on brand safety. Its core differentiator is the integration of AI-driven threat detection with an automated takedown infrastructure. While competitors may only alert you to phishing domains or fake apps, Bolster actively engages with registrars, app stores, and social media platforms to remove these threats at scale.

The platform monitors multiple vectors including domains, mobile app stores, social media profiles, and the dark web. It uses machine learning to distinguish between legitimate brand activity and malicious impersonation, achieving what it claims is 99.999% accuracy in takedowns. This "detection-to-remediation" loop is designed to reduce the time from threat identification to neutralization, often within seconds for high-confidence signals.

For security teams, Bolster serves as a force multiplier. It integrates with existing SIEMs and SOARs to feed threat intelligence into broader security operations. By automating the labor-intensive process of monitoring and reporting abuse, it allows organizations to protect their brand reputation and customer trust without maintaining a large manual monitoring team.

## Pricing model

Bolster does not publish public pricing tiers. The model is subscription-based and tailored to enterprise needs. Prospective customers are directed to "Request a Demo" to receive a custom quote. Pricing typically depends on the scope of monitoring (number of domains, social accounts, etc.) and the volume of takedown actions required. This opaque pricing model is common in specialized B2B security services but contrasts with the transparent, self-serve metering of hyperscalers.
