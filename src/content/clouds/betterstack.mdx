## What makes BetterStack different

BetterStack consolidates observability and incident response into a single platform with transparent, predictable pricing that undercuts Datadog by up to 30x. The core differentiator is aggressive cost structure combined with no data sampling—ingest 1 TB of logs, traces, and metrics per month for $687 versus Datadog's estimated $55,574. This appeals to teams that want enterprise-grade observability without the bill shock.

The platform is OpenTelemetry-native from the ground up, meaning tracing, metrics, and logs flow through standardized APIs rather than proprietary SDKs. The AI SRE feature uses Claude-powered agentic workflows to automate incident triage and root cause analysis, reducing mean time to resolution. eBPF-based service mapping captures application topology without code instrumentation, and incident management integrates directly into Slack and Microsoft Teams for faster resolution workflows.

## Pricing model

BetterStack uses a straightforward usage-based model with bundled tiers. The example given is:

- **1 TB logs/month + 1 TB traces/month + 1 TB metrics/month**: $687/month (annual billing)
- Incident management includes 1 responder with the Tera bundle
- Pricing is significantly lower if you accept European data location

Key distinction: no "custom metrics" surcharge, no indexed span penalties, and no sampling-driven tiering. You pay for what you ingest, not what you query. This flattens the cost curve for high-volume observability compared to Datadog's dimensional metrics pricing or Grafana Cloud's carved-out buckets.

## When it fits

- **Cost-sensitive teams migrating from Datadog.** BetterStack offers migration assistance and will cover the remaining balance of your Datadog contract.
- **Organizations running OpenTelemetry already.** Native OTEL support means no instrumentation translation layer.
- **Incident-heavy workflows.** Slack/Teams integration and AI-powered incident triage reduce context switching and MTTR.
- **Full-stack observability without tool sprawl.** Logs, traces, metrics, uptime, error tracking, and status pages in one place eliminates multi-vendor fatigue.
- **Startups and mid-market teams.** Predictable, sub-$1k-per-month pricing removes the "surprise bill" risk common at scale.

## When it doesn't

- **Hyperscale environments with strict data residency.** BetterStack's default European region may not suit teams requiring US-only or multi-region compliance.
- **Complex custom metrics or advanced alerting DSLs.** If your team has built deeply into Datadog's custom metrics or terraform-heavy alert rules, migration friction is non-trivial.
