## What makes Heap different

Heap eliminates the need for developers to manually define and instrument tracking events. Instead, it automatically captures every user interaction on web and mobile—clicks, form fills, page views, and custom events—creating a retroactive event taxonomy that product teams can query without code. This "capture first, analyze later" approach means teams never lose data and can explore user behavior that wasn't anticipated at instrumentation time.

The platform pairs automatic capture with AI-powered analytics through **Sense AI**, which surfaces insights and answers questions in natural language. For teams drowning in behavioral data, **Heap Illuminate** applies data science techniques to identify unknown friction points and conversion bottlenecks. Integration with Contentsquare's Digital Experience Analytics adds session replay and heatmap context in a single interface, bridging product analytics with qualitative user behavior understanding.

## Pricing model

Heap uses a usage-based pricing model tied to tracked events and users. The platform offers a **free tier** for teams starting out, with paid tiers scaling based on monthly event volume and active user counts. Specific per-unit pricing is not published publicly; teams must request a demo or trial to see custom quotes. This approach appeals to early-stage companies (free tier) while scaling costs with business growth. Unlike fixed-tier SaaS analytics tools, usage-based pricing aligns Heap's revenue with customer success—the more events you capture, the more value you're likely extracting.

## When it fits

- **Product-led growth (PLG) teams** that need retroactive cohort analysis without pre-instrumentation planning.
- **E-commerce and conversion-focused sites** looking to optimize funnels with session replay and heatmap context.
- **Cross-functional teams** (product, marketing, data) who benefit from no-code analytics interfaces that don't require SQL or analytics engineering.
- **Enterprise SaaS** seeking comprehensive journey mapping and AI-assisted insights at scale.
- **Mobile app teams** needing unified analytics across iOS, Android, and web properties.

## When it doesn't

Heap is a **product analytics platform**, not a general-purpose cloud provider. It does not offer compute, storage, networking, or infrastructure services. Organizations seeking IaaS/PaaS alternatives to AWS, GCP, or Azure should not consider Heap.
