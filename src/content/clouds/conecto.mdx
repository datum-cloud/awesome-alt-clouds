## What makes Conecto different

Conecto is not an infrastructure cloud provider — it's a customer messaging platform built on top of Google Cloud that bundles live chat, an AI chatbot, shared inbox, CRM, ticketing, and analytics into a single product. Rather than competing with hyperscalers on compute or storage, it targets the fragmentation problem support and sales teams face when stitching together separate chat, helpdesk, CRM, and bot tools.

A key architectural choice is that its AI agent only answers from content teams explicitly crawl and approve from their own website and docs — it does not generate free-form answers, and it hands off to a human agent with full conversation history when uncertain. Voice is built directly into the same chat widget (not a separate product or add-on), falling back to text automatically when microphone access isn't available, and answers come from the same knowledge base used by the text chatbot so responses stay consistent across channels.

## Pricing model

Conecto uses flat, published USD subscription pricing per workspace, with AI usage metered via credits:

- **Free** — $0/mo forever: 2 seats, 1 chat widget, 100 CRM contacts, no AI credits.
- **Lite** — $25/mo (14 days free trial): 4 seats, 3 widgets, 500 CRM contacts, 5 AI credits/mo then pay-as-you-go; unlocks AI agent, bots, ticketing, routing.
- **Scale** — $65/mo: 10 seats, 10 widgets, 1,000 CRM contacts, 15 AI credits/mo.
- **Max** — $215/mo: 20+ seats (additional seats $10/mo each), 50 widgets, 7,000 CRM contacts, 50 AI credits/mo.
- **Enterprise** — custom seats/credits, SSO, dedicated success manager (contact sales).

What stands out versus typical cloud pricing: no per-seat metering on lower tiers, no per-resolution charges, and AI overage priced in plain USD credits rather than opaque token math — pricing tables and feature comparisons are published openly on the site.

## When it fits

- Support teams wanting to consolidate live chat, ticketing, and a shared inbox into one tool instead of multiple point solutions.
- Teams that want an AI chatbot strictly grounded in their own approved content (docs/website), with human handoff for anything it can't answer.
- Small-to-midsize sales/support orgs (roughly 2–20+ seats) that need built-in CRM and analytics without integrating separate systems.
- Businesses needing multilingual (50+ languages) self-service via chat, voice, and a help center.
- Teams wanting predictable, transparent monthly pricing rather than usage-based infrastructure billing.

## When it doesn't

- Not suitable as general-purpose cloud infrastructure (compute, storage, networking) — it's a vertical SaaS product for customer messaging only.
- Enterprises needing very high seat counts, custom compliance, or deep on-prem/self-hosted deployment may need the Enterprise tier or a different platform entirely.

## Inclusion criteria

Conecto meets all 3 inclusion criteria: transparent, published pricing tiers at conecto.chat/pricing; self-service signup via a free plan and 14-day trial (no card required) at conecto.chat/get-started; and infrastructure/security trust indicators (Google Cloud hosting, Stripe-secured billing, encryption in transit) referenced on the homepage.
