## What makes Bluedot different

Bluedot takes a fundamentally different approach to meeting capture than established competitors like Otter.ai or Fireflies. Instead of deploying a bot participant that joins calls, Bluedot uses browser extensions and desktop applications to record from the participant's device—eliminating the need for host permissions, calendar access, or bot notifications.

This bot-free architecture makes Bluedot non-intrusive: participants don't see an extra attendee in the meeting roster, and the recording happens discretely. The product works across any platform (Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, Webex, Slack huddles, phone calls, or in-person meetings via mobile app) without special permissions. For teams concerned about meeting etiquette or psychological comfort, this is a meaningful differentiator.

On the AI side, Bluedot emphasizes accuracy with technical terminology, abbreviations, and speaker identification. The platform also integrates directly with CRMs and ATS systems via API and MCP (Model Context Protocol), enabling post-meeting automation like automatic note sync and email follow-ups.

## Pricing model

Bluedot offers a **free tier** that includes 5 lifetime recordings, allowing users to test core features before committing. The specific paid tier pricing is not published on the public website, but the freemium model with usage-based trial caps suggests a subscription structure likely tiered by monthly recording limits or team seat count.

Users can upgrade beyond the free tier for unlimited recording and transcription. The model stands out for lowering the barrier to entry—no credit card required for meaningful evaluation.

## When it fits

- **Sales teams** automating CRM updates (Salesforce, HubSpot) and follow-up workflows post-call
- **Recruiters & talent acquisition** who want to focus on candidates rather than notetaking, with automatic ATS sync
- **Customer success** managing renewal calls and building a searchable record of all customer interactions
- **Engineering teams** documenting technical decisions and onboarding knowledge without a meeting bot disrupting calls
- **Founders & solo entrepreneurs** who need lightweight meeting capture without infrastructure overhead

## When it doesn't

Bluedot is not suitable for enterprises requiring on-premises deployment, air-gapped environments, or strict data residency guarantees in specific regions. It is also not a fit for teams that need real-time collaborative notetaking during meetings (it focuses on post-call summaries rather than live collaborative editing).
