## What makes Plausible different

Plausible is built explicitly as a lightweight, privacy-first alternative to Google Analytics. Unlike GA4—which requires cookie banners, loads heavy JavaScript, and sends data to Google's infrastructure—Plausible uses a minimal script (~1 KB), requires no consent management, and stores all data on EU-owned servers where it never leaves the continent. The entire codebase is public and auditable, allowing customers to self-host on their own infrastructure or use Plausible's managed SaaS version.

The platform prioritizes actionable simplicity: all essential metrics (pageviews, bounce rate, traffic sources, goals, revenue) appear on a single dashboard with no training required. It automatically filters bots, tracks scroll depth without code, integrates Google Search Console for organic search insights, and supports ecommerce revenue tracking—all designed to eliminate the complexity that makes GA4 difficult for most site owners.

## Pricing model

Plausible uses a subscription model with transparent monthly pricing based on monthly pageviews. Plans start with a free 30-day trial requiring no credit card. While exact current pricing was not detailed in the available materials, the provider is clear that pricing is visible on their [pricing page](https://plausible.io/#pricing) and that they operate on straightforward, predictable billing—a contrast to Google Analytics' opaque data collection and processing costs.

The model is designed to reward sites that are selective about analytics: smaller publishers and creators pay proportionally less than they would for enterprise GA4 implementations, while high-traffic sites benefit from capped costs at scale.

## When it fits

- **Privacy-conscious websites & publishers**: When GDPR/CCPA compliance and avoiding cookie banners matters, or when you want analytics that don't require explicit visitor consent.
- **Simple, focused analytics needs**: Small-to-medium ecommerce sites, SaaS products, and content creators who need goals, revenue tracking, and traffic sources—but not 200+ custom events and complex ML features.
- **Agencies and white-label use cases**: Plausible offers white-label dashboards, making it ideal for agencies bundling analytics into client offerings.
- **Lightweight performance requirements**: When page speed and Core Web Vitals matter; Plausible's ~1 KB script has negligible impact compared to GA4 or similar tools.
- **EU data residency requirements**: Organizations that must keep analytics data within the EU for regulatory or contractual reasons.

## When it doesn't

Plausible is not a fit for organizations needing advanced machine learning, predictive analytics, or deeply custom event schemas. It also lacks the breadth of integrations (ad platforms, CRM systems, data warehousing) that GA4 or enterprise tools provide.
