## What makes FullStory different

FullStory specializes in _digital experience analytics_—capturing real user behavior and session context to power AI-driven insights and interventions. Unlike generic data warehouses, FullStory combines session replay, product analytics, and AI agents (StoryAI) to connect product friction directly to business outcomes. The platform is "private by default," with a focus on compliance and semantic accessibility, allowing teams to search and analyze user journeys using human-readable element names rather than pixel coordinates.

The company's differentiation lies in its "anywhere" data ecosystem: FullStory enriches behavioral data in real time, integrating with existing tech stacks to activate insights (personalization, support acceleration, fraud detection) without requiring data export. This positions it as a behavioral analytics and activation layer rather than a raw compute or storage provider.

## Pricing model

FullStory operates on a **subscription model** with tiered plans available on its [Plans & Pricing page](https://www.fullstory.com/plans/). Specific pricing numbers are not publicly disclosed in the provided materials; pricing typically varies by data volume (sessions captured) and features selected. The platform emphasizes transparent, self-service signup with demo availability for larger or custom deployments.

## When it fits

- **E-commerce and SaaS companies** optimizing checkout flows, sign-up friction, and cart abandonment—FullStory's customers include Chipotle, Pizza Hut, VividSeats, and Zipcar.
- **Product and engineering teams** needing session-level context to debug errors and understand user intent beyond aggregate metrics.
- **Customer support teams** resolving tickets faster by reviewing actual user sessions alongside support interactions.
- **Industries requiring compliance and fraud detection**: travel, hospitality, financial services, and gaming can use real-time signals and journey maps to identify risk and prevent fraud.
- **Companies building AI-driven experiences** who need rich behavioral data to train and improve AI agents (StoryAI).

## When it doesn't

FullStory is not a general-purpose cloud infrastructure provider—it is a vertical analytics platform. Organizations needing compute, storage, databases, or broad IaaS capabilities should use AWS, GCP, or Azure. High-volume, cost-sensitive batch analytics workloads may find cheaper alternatives in dedicated data warehouse services.
