## What makes Stonly different

Stonly is not a traditional cloud infrastructure provider; it is a specialized SaaS application for customer service knowledge management. Unlike hyperscalers that provide raw compute or storage, Stonly focuses entirely on structuring and delivering organizational knowledge to both support agents and end-users. Its core differentiator is the "Interactive Guide" format, which allows support teams to create step-by-step, contextual workflows that guide users through complex troubleshooting or onboarding processes, rather than relying on static text articles.

The platform integrates AI directly into the knowledge workflow. Its "AI Answers" feature uses a conversational bot that pulls responses strictly from verified, structured knowledge bases, reducing hallucinations common in generic LLM implementations. For internal agents, Stonly provides "Agent Assist" tools that surface relevant guides and decision trees in real-time during ticket handling, aiming to reduce resolution times and agent errors.

Stonly is designed to sit on top of existing CRM and helpdesk ecosystems rather than replace them. It offers deep integrations with Zendesk, Salesforce, Freshdesk, and ServiceNow, acting as the intelligent layer that powers self-service and agent productivity within those established workflows.

## Pricing model

Stonly operates on a subscription-based pricing model. Specific tiered pricing numbers (e.g., cost per user or per month) are not publicly listed on the homepage and require requesting a demo or contacting sales. However, the platform is generally structured around seat-based licensing for agents and potentially usage-based or site-based metrics for customer-facing widgets. This contrasts with typical cloud providers that charge for compute hours or data egress; Stonly’s cost is tied to the value of support efficiency and ticket deflection.

## When it fits

-   **High-Volume Support Teams:** Organizations looking to deflect tickets through effective self-service portals.
-   **Complex Products:** Businesses with intricate software or hardware that require step-by-step interactive troubleshooting rather than simple FAQ answers.
-   **Regulated Industries:** Companies in healthcare or finance requiring strict version control, access rights, and compliance certifications like SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, and HIPAA.
-   **Existing Helpdesk Users:** Teams already using Zendesk, Salesforce, or ServiceNow who want to enhance their knowledge base without migrating platforms.

## When it doesn't

Stonly is not suitable for workloads requiring infrastructure management, such as hosting applications, managing databases, or running machine learning training jobs. It is an application layer tool, not a cloud provider.
