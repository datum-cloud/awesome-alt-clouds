## What makes Makora different

Makora positions itself as an optimization layer rather than a traditional cloud provider — instead of just renting GPU capacity, it ships specialized automated agents across the entire inference stack (kernel-level, scheduling, orchestration) to extract more performance from the same hardware. The company claims up to 5x throughput improvements and up to 70% reductions in time-to-first-token, achieved without requiring customers to rewrite model code.

A notable architectural piece is the orchestrator layer, which routes requests across models, regions, and replicas in real time, balancing latency, cost, and reliability — described as automating decisions a senior infra engineer would otherwise make manually. This differs from hyperscaler inference offerings, which typically expose fixed instance types and leave optimization work to the customer.

Makora also supports multiple frontier and open models (e.g., Kimi K2.6, GLM 5.1, DeepSeek v4) and offers hardware-agnostic custom kernel engineering across NVIDIA, AMD, Google TPU, AWS Trainium, and Qualcomm silicon, positioning itself against lock-in to any single hardware vendor.

## Pricing model

Makora uses usage-based, per-token pricing for its hosted models, with published rates such as:

- Input: $0.09–$2.55 per million tokens (varies by model)
- Output: $0.20–$12.75 per million tokens
- Cached read: $0.02–$0.26 per million tokens

It also offers subscription tiers layered on top of usage: a $20/month Starter plan and a $200/month Developer plan (both listed as sold out at time of writing), which include unlimited access to models under 40B parameters plus quotas for larger models, concurrent request limits, and a 10% pay-as-you-go discount. Enterprise/dedicated inference and on-prem deployments are quoted custom. Overflow beyond subscription quotas rolls into pay-as-you-go billing rather than hard-blocking usage — a departure from rigid hyperscaler quota systems.

## When it fits

- Teams running high-volume LLM inference who want automatic speed/cost gains without re-engineering their serving stack
- Applications sensitive to time-to-first-token latency (e.g., interactive agents, coding assistants)
- Organizations wanting to serve fine-tuned or custom models optimized for specific hardware (including non-NVIDIA silicon)
- Enterprises needing dedicated or on-prem inference deployments with custom kernel tuning
- Developers who want simple per-token pricing on frontier and open-weight models without managing GPU infrastructure

## When it doesn't

- Workloads needing general-purpose compute, storage, or non-AI cloud infrastructure (Makora is inference-focused only)
- Teams wanting full training infrastructure rather than inference serving

## Inclusion criteria

Makora meets all three inclusion criteria: transparent usage-based pricing is published per model (input/output/cached-read rates) at makora.com; self-service signup and trial access is available via app.makora.com; and subscription tiers plus a public product/status presence support the self-service and SLA criteria.
