## What makes NetActuate different

NetActuate positions itself as an edge-first alternative to hyperscaler dominance, with a deliberate focus on geographic distribution and low-latency compute. The platform spans 45+ global edge locations—far more granular than AWS or Azure's traditional regional model—enabling workloads to run closer to end users without the cost and vendor lock-in penalties of major cloud providers.

The provider emphasizes hybrid cloud integration: workloads can seamlessly span NetActuate's public cloud, private dedicated hardware, colocation facilities, and on-premises equipment in a unified management plane. This is particularly valuable for organizations migrating away from hyperscalers or managing latency-sensitive applications (gaming, real-time inference, content delivery). NetActuate also partners with specialized hardware vendors like NETINT for VPU-accelerated inference and Synamedia for edge CDN caching, avoiding the bundled-service model of mainstream clouds.

Their network-first architecture—built on BGP Anycast, global IP transit, and IXP peering—differentiates them for networking-heavy workloads like DNS, DDoS mitigation, and content distribution, where traditional cloud providers charge premium rates.

## Pricing model

NetActuate uses a usage-based pricing model aligned with consumption (compute hours, bandwidth, storage), but specific per-unit rates are not published on their public website. Instead, they emphasize transparent, custom quoting and positioning their pricing as lower than hyperscalers for equivalent edge services—particularly around bandwidth and bare-metal provisioning. The ability to mix public cloud VMs, private dedicated hardware, and colocation in one bill reduces the need to juggle multiple vendor invoices.

## When it fits

- **Edge inference & real-time ML**: Running models at 45+ edge locations with NETINT VPU acceleration for sub-millisecond latency.
- **Content delivery & video streaming**: Global CDN, Anycast DNS, and DDoS mitigation without hyperscaler markup.
- **Hybrid & multi-cloud workloads**: Seamlessly integrate cloud resources with on-prem or colocated hardware in a single control plane.
- **Cloud repatriation**: Cost-effectively migrate away from AWS/GCP/Azure while maintaining geographic redundancy.
- **High-bandwidth networking**: BGP Anycast, IP transit, and IXP peering for operators and telecom use cases.

## When it doesn't

NetActuate is not ideal for organizations deeply invested in AWS-native services (RDS, Lambda, SageMaker) or requiring industry-specific compliance templates pre-built into the console. Smaller startups may find the custom-quote sales model less frictionless than AWS's instant provisioning.

## Inclusion criteria

NetActuate meets all three criteria:

1. **Transparent pricing**: Usage-based model published; self-service calculator and custom quotes available at [netactuate.com](https://netactuate.com/).
2. **Self-service signup**: Instant portal access and VM provisioning from 45+ edge locations via their customer portal.
3. **Public SLA & status page**: Infrastructure redundancy, RTO/RPO metrics, and service-level commitments documented in their solutions and technical resources.
