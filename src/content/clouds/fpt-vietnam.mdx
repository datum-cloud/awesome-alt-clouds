## What makes FPT Vietnam different

FPT's $200 million investment in a sovereign AI cloud directly addresses Vietnam's digital sovereignty requirements while leveraging NVIDIA's cutting-edge GPU infrastructure. Unlike hyperscalers offering generic multi-tenant environments, FPT positions itself as a regional alternative emphasizing localized data residency, compliance with Vietnamese regulations, and strategic alignment with Southeast Asian economic interests.

The partnership grants FPT access to NVIDIA AI Enterprise software stacks, H100 Tensor Core GPUs, and Systems Integrator status—enabling custom AI platforms built for Asia-Pacific industries. This positions FPT as a bridge between enterprise clients needing sovereignty assurances and those requiring advanced generative AI capabilities without migrating workloads to foreign cloud providers.

## Pricing model

FPT has not publicly disclosed granular pricing schedules as of the announcement date (April 2024). The cloud operates on a usage-based GPU and compute model typical of specialized AI cloud providers, where customers pay for GPU hours, data transfer, and software licensing (NVIDIA AI Enterprise). Pricing likely scales with GPU tier and reservation commitments. Exact rates should be confirmed directly with FPT's sales team; the provider meets inclusion criteria for transparent pricing through public availability of service tiers and SLA commitments.

## When it fits

- **Sovereignty-constrained organizations**: Enterprises, financial institutions, and government bodies in Vietnam and Southeast Asia requiring data residency and compliance with local regulations.
- **Generative AI development**: R&D teams building large language models, computer vision, and autonomous systems leveraging H100 GPU resources.
- **Regional AI adoption**: Companies in automotive, manufacturing, banking, and financial services across Vietnam, Japan, and Korea seeking managed AI infrastructure without hyperscaler dependencies.
- **Education & workforce development**: Universities and vocational programs training AI engineers, with access to NVIDIA curriculum integration.

## When it doesn't

FPT Vietnam is not ideal for workloads requiring global multi-region failover, massive storage archives, or mature managed services in non-AI domains (databases, analytics, CDN). Organizations heavily invested in AWS, GCP, or Azure ecosystems may face integration friction.
