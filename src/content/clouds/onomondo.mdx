## What makes Onomondo different

Onomondo distinguishes itself from hyperscalers by specializing exclusively in global IoT connectivity rather than offering a broad suite of general-purpose cloud infrastructure. While AWS IoT Core or Azure IoT Hub require you to manage SIM provisioning, carrier negotiations, and roaming agreements separately, Onomondo provides a unified "single global SIM" solution. This allows developers to deploy devices in over 180 countries using the same hardware and firmware configuration, eliminating the complexity of managing multiple local carriers and data contracts.

The platform’s core differentiator is its network-agnostic approach combined with deep visibility. By leveraging SoftSIM and eSIM technologies (including SGP.32), Onomondo enables devices to automatically select the strongest available network without carrier lock-in. This is paired with self-service diagnostic tools that provide packet-level insights and signaling logs, allowing teams to troubleshoot connectivity issues without submitting support tickets. This level of transparency and control is typically reserved for enterprise-level telecom operators, making it highly accessible for IoT product teams.

Unlike traditional MNOs (Mobile Network Operators) that often bury costs in complex roaming fees or minimum monthly commitments, Onomondo operates on a transparent, usage-based model. There are no charges for inactive SIMs, and billing is consolidated into a single invoice regardless of the device's location. This aligns costs directly with fleet activity, reducing the financial risk associated with scaling IoT deployments across new markets.

## Pricing model

Onomondo utilizes a transparent, pay-as-you-go pricing model tailored for IoT fleets. Key characteristics include:

- **Data Usage:** Customers pay only for the data actually consumed by active SIMs.
- **No Inactive SIM Fees:** There are no monthly platform fees or charges for SIMs that are not currently transmitting data.
- **No Roaming Surcharges:** The global coverage is integrated into the base rate, avoiding unexpected roaming charges.
- **Unified Billing:** A single invoice covers the entire global fleet, simplifying financial operations.

Specific per-MB pricing tiers are not publicly listed on the general website and require contacting sales for a quote based on volume. However, the model is designed to be predictable, with no hidden markups or bill shocks, standing in contrast to traditional telecom contracts that often include high minimums and punitive overage fees.

## When it fits

- **Global IoT Deployments:** Products that need to operate seamlessly across multiple countries without re-engineering connectivity stacks for each region.
- **High-Growth Startups:** Teams that need to scale from dozens to thousands of devices without negotiating new carrier contracts at each step.
- **Fleet Management:** Use cases requiring real-time visibility into device status, network selection, and data usage to prevent downtime.
- **Hard-to-Reach Areas:** Devices deployed in regions where local carrier negotiations are difficult or time-consuming.

## When it doesn't

- **General Cloud Compute/Storage:** Onomondo does not offer virtual machines, object storage, or serverless compute functions.
- **On-Premises Private Networks:** While they offer Private Wireless Network Core, this is for cellular connectivity, not general-purpose on-prem data centers.
