## What makes Obvious different

Obvious positions itself as a productivity-focused AI agent rather than a conversational chatbot. The core differentiator is collaborative artifact generation—the agent doesn't just answer questions, it actively builds deliverables (documents, spreadsheets, code, presentations) alongside users. This shifts the paradigm from "ask AI, get an answer" to "work with AI as a teammate."

The platform emphasizes role-specific use cases: lifecycle marketers, data migrators, content creators, recruiters, founders, and real estate professionals. Each persona receives tailored templates and shortcuts that understand domain-specific workflows. The agent integrates directly into existing tools rather than forcing users into a standalone interface, supporting on-demand integrations with systems teams already use daily.

## Pricing model

Obvious offers transparent, tiered subscription pricing with a clear free tier available via self-service signup. Specific tier pricing is not detailed in the available content, but the model positions itself as straightforward subscription-based access to the agent platform. The pricing page exists at https://obvious.ai/pricing and enterprise plans are available for larger organizations.

Unlike consumption-based cloud pricing, Obvious uses a seat/usage subscription model typical of SaaS productivity tools rather than per-API-call metering.

## When it fits

- **Content production workflows**: Writers, marketers, and creators who need stylistically consistent output that matches their voice and brand standards.
- **Data-heavy operational tasks**: Teams managing data migrations, schema mapping, and ETL processes where edge case handling and validation are critical.
- **Cross-functional collaboration**: Organizations wanting AI assistance embedded in team spaces (via @mentions and shared boards) rather than isolated in a separate tool.
- **Template-driven processes**: Companies with repeatable workflows (campaign planning, financial analysis, hiring pipelines) that benefit from domain-specific shortcuts.
- **Tool consolidation**: Teams frustrated with context-switching between AI chat, document editors, and automation platforms.

## When it doesn't

Obvious is not designed for compute-intensive workloads, distributed systems architecture, or applications requiring custom infrastructure provisioning. It's a productivity application, not a cloud compute or infrastructure platform.

## Inclusion criteria

Obvious meets all three inclusion criteria for alt-cloud.org:

1. **Transparent pricing**: Pricing page publicly available at https://obvious.ai/pricing with subscription tiers clearly outlined.
2. **Self-service signup**: Free account creation available at https://app.obvious.ai/signup with immediate platform access.
3. **Public SLA/status**: Security certifications (ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II) and compliance details (GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA) are publicly documented on the website, demonstrating service reliability and operational standards.
