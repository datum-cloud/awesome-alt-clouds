## What makes Jottacloud different

Jottacloud operates as a privacy-first alternative to US-based cloud storage by storing all data exclusively in Norway, where it benefits from the country's strict data protection regulations and independence from US surveillance frameworks. Unlike AWS, Google Drive, or Dropbox—which operate globally distributed infrastructure—Jottacloud's single-jurisdiction model appeals to users and organizations seeking data sovereignty and compliance with European privacy standards.

The service emphasizes sustainability by running entirely on 100% renewable energy, and focuses on consumer-friendly features like automatic backup, version history (retaining five previous versions), and photo-centric organization rather than competing on enterprise infrastructure scale. With 1.9 million users, it occupies a niche between consumer file sync tools and enterprise cloud platforms.

## Pricing model

Jottacloud offers transparent, straightforward subscription plans:

- **Free**: 5 GB storage, single user
- **Unlimited**: $11.99/month ($119.90/year) — unlimited storage with gradually reduced upload speed after 5 TB
- **Home 1 TB**: $6.99/month ($69.90/year) — 1 TB shared across 5 users, no bandwidth limits
- **Pro 10 TB**: $29.90/month ($299/year) — 10 TB, commercial license, priority support, maximum speed

Annual billing offers ~17% discount versus monthly. The pricing is significantly lower than Dropbox's comparable tiers and avoids the hyperscaler pricing complexity of AWS S3 or Google Cloud Storage, making capacity planning predictable for individual users and small families.

## When it fits

- **Privacy-conscious individuals** seeking data residency in Norway and GDPR/Nordic privacy compliance without US-based cloud giants
- **European users** wanting to avoid transatlantic data transfers and US government data access requests
- **Photo and media backup** for families or individuals preferring automatic backup with built-in version history over manual sync
- **Compliance-driven organizations** in regulated sectors (healthcare, financial services) operating in the EU and needing to demonstrate data sovereignty
- **Sustainability-focused users** prioritizing 100% renewable energy operations

## When it doesn't

Jottacloud is not suited for enterprise workloads requiring global multi-region replication, API-driven infrastructure-as-a-service capabilities, or integration with AWS/GCP ecosystems. It lacks the compute, database, networking, and managed services that modern cloud platforms provide.
