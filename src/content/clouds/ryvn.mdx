## What makes Ryvn different

Ryvn solves a critical gap in SaaS sales: the "Can you deploy to our cloud?" question that blocks enterprise deals. Rather than building a traditional multi-tenant cloud platform, Ryvn provides a single control plane to orchestrate deployments, updates, and observability across customer-owned infrastructure—whether AWS, GCP, Azure, Kubernetes, or on-premises.

The platform is built explicitly for the BYOC (Bring Your Own Cloud) model, eliminating the operational burden vendors typically face supporting disparate customer environments. Ryvn customers—mostly early-stage AI and fintech startups—can offer deployment flexibility without hiring dedicated infrastructure teams. The zero-trust architecture ensures customer data never leaves their own cloud, addressing compliance concerns that are common blockers in regulated industries like healthcare and finance.

Built by former infrastructure engineers from Palantir, Vercel, and Capital One, Ryvn emphasizes observability and debuggability without requiring vendor access to customer systems. This focus on transparency and control resonates strongly with enterprise buyers.

## When it fits

- **SaaS vendors pursuing enterprise customers** who require BYOC or on-premises deployment options but lack dedicated DevOps teams.
- **Regulated industries** (healthcare, fintech, government) where data residency and zero-trust security are non-negotiable.
- **Multi-tenant SaaS platforms** needing to support customer-specific infrastructure without operational overhead.
- **Startups competing on flexibility** and willing to offer deployment optionality as a sales differentiator against larger incumbents.
- **Teams using Kubernetes, Docker, or Terraform** who want a unified control plane across heterogeneous environments.

## When it doesn't

Ryvn is not a general-purpose infrastructure provider and does not replace AWS, GCP, or Azure. It is purpose-built for vendors managing their own customer deployments, not for teams looking to host applications directly on shared cloud resources at scale.
