## What makes Valarian different

Valarian builds a sovereignty layer that sits between frontier AI models and the customer's own environment, rather than offering compute infrastructure itself. Its core product, ACRA, ensures that prompts, context, and outputs are encrypted and sealed within the customer's VPC or air-gapped perimeter — the model only "reads" the prompt outside that perimeter in a controlled, policy-enforced way, with no external inference endpoint ever retaining data. This architecture lets customers run frontier-class models (hosted on AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud) while keeping full custody of keys, data, and audit trails in-country.

Unlike hyperscalers, which require trusting a third party with inference infrastructure, Valarian's positioning is explicitly built for privileged government and defence workloads that need air-gapped deployment with in-country key custody, and for regulated enterprises (trading, communications) that require end-to-end auditability of every model access. The company was founded by individuals with backgrounds at the UK Ministry of Defence, British Army, US Special Forces, and Darktrace, and is "Made in the UK" — reflecting a design philosophy oriented around national security and compliance rather than general-purpose cloud scale.

Rather than competing on raw compute price or breadth of services, Valarian differentiates on policy enforcement at the point of inference: every retrieval, tool call, and output is logged (e.g., "PERMIT retrieval," "DENY web fetch," "SEAL output") giving customers granular control and auditability that standard hyperscaler AI services don't natively provide.
