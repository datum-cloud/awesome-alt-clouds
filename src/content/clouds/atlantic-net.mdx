## What makes Atlantic.net different

Atlantic.net positions itself as a security-first cloud provider with over 30 years of hosting experience, specifically targeting compliance-heavy industries. Unlike hyperscalers that offer compliance as an add-on, Atlantic.net builds security into their infrastructure foundation with SOC 2/3 audited data centers, HIPAA/HITECH auditing, and turn-key compliance solutions for healthcare and financial services.

The provider emphasizes high-touch support and managed services over self-service automation, offering 24/7/365 access to dedicated support veterans. Their infrastructure spans eight international data centers with a focus on redundancy and their advertised 100% uptime SLA. They've also partnered with NVIDIA to offer dedicated GPU infrastructure specifically designed for AI/ML workloads and large language model training.

## Pricing model

Atlantic.net uses a mixed pricing model across their service portfolio. Their cloud platform appears to offer hourly billing for compute instances, while dedicated servers and bare metal hosting likely use monthly pricing. GPU hosting and compliance-ready infrastructure are positioned as premium offerings with custom pricing based on requirements.

The provider emphasizes consultation-driven pricing rather than transparent self-service pricing, encouraging potential customers to contact sales at 866-618-DATA for tailored hosting environments. This approach aligns with their managed services focus but differs from typical cloud providers' transparent, self-service pricing models.

## When it fits

- Healthcare organizations requiring HIPAA/HITECH compliance with Business Associate Agreements
- Financial services needing PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure with managed security
- AI/ML workloads requiring dedicated GPU compute with NVIDIA acceleration
- Enterprises wanting high-touch managed services over self-service cloud management
- Organizations prioritizing security auditing and compliance certifications over cost optimization

## When it doesn't

Atlantic.net is likely overkill for simple web applications or startups needing cost-effective, self-service cloud resources without compliance requirements.

## Inclusion criteria

Atlantic.net meets all three inclusion criteria: they offer transparent service descriptions on their website, provide self-service signup capabilities, and prominently advertise their 100% uptime SLA as a key differentiator across their marketing materials.
