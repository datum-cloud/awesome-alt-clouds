## What makes Hivelocity different

Hivelocity emphasizes human-first customer support as a core differentiator, claiming the highest transactional NPS in the industry with 24/7/365 availability. Unlike hyperscalers that route support through automated systems, Hivelocity positions itself as a partner rather than a transactional vendor.

The platform unifies hybrid infrastructure management—bare metal, colocation, and VPS—through a single control plane with SSO and SAML integration. This matters for organizations that don't fit cleanly into a single deployment model, allowing them to mix hardware ownership (colocation), leased servers, and virtual instances without juggling separate management interfaces.

With 40+ data centers across 6 continents, Hivelocity offers geographic diversity comparable to major providers but without the hyperscaler abstraction layers. Developers get direct API access, a Terraform provider, and documented endpoints for infrastructure automation.

## Pricing model

Hivelocity publishes tiered pricing accessible at `hivelocity.net/pricing`, with dedicated servers, VPS, and colocation quoted separately. Dedicated servers are billed monthly; VPS offerings support hourly and monthly billing. The site advertises "50% off Dedicated Servers" promotions but does not publish baseline rates in the provided excerpt.

Pricing is location-dependent, with separate quote pages for each of the 40+ regions. This regional transparency differs from hyperscalers' zone-based flat-rate models, enabling cost optimization for geographically distributed workloads.

## When it fits

- **Bare metal workloads** needing full hardware control without cloud abstraction—gaming, HFT, crypto validators, AI inference.
- **Hybrid deployments** combining colocation (bring your own hardware), dedicated servers, and VPS under one management interface.
- **Regulated industries** (fintech, healthcare) requiring SOC 2, PCI DSS, HIPAA, and GDPR compliance with transparent, auditable infrastructure.
- **Global edge latency requirements** with preferred presence in specific regions across Americas, Europe, Africa, and Asia-Pacific.
- **Hands-on support needs** where human escalation and expertise matter more than self-service documentation.

## When it doesn't

Hivelocity is not a managed Kubernetes or serverless platform; it excels at lower-level infrastructure but requires more operational overhead than fully managed services. Workloads expecting auto-scaling, event-driven compute, or sub-minute provisioning are better served by hyperscalers.
