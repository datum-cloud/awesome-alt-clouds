## What makes Voltage Park different

Voltage Park specializes in bare-metal GPU infrastructure purpose-built for large-scale AI training. Unlike general-purpose cloud providers, it focuses exclusively on deep learning workloads with direct hardware access—eliminating the virtualization overhead that can slow model training. The infrastructure spans NVIDIA HGX H100 GPUs alongside newer Hopper, Blackwell, and Grace Blackwell architectures, all interconnected by NVIDIA Quantum-2 InfiniBand offering 3.2 Tbps aggregate bandwidth. This architecture enables 40% faster large language model training compared to virtualized alternatives.

The provider attracts leading AI labs and scaling startups (Phind, Luma AI, Covariant, Cursor, Atomic AI, Runware, and others) by removing cloud complexity while preserving performance. Both on-demand and reserved instance models are available, with contracts aligned to long-term ROI rather than lock-in.

## Pricing model

**On-Demand:** Starts at $1.99/hour for single-node compute. Self-serve provisioning spins up instances in 15 minutes with no long-term contracts.

**Dedicated Reserve:** 12+ month contracts available with flexible payment terms optimized for teams building production AI systems. Exact tiered pricing for reserved capacity is not published on the website; contacting sales is required for volume discounts.

The hourly on-demand rate is competitive for bare-metal GPU access and undercuts typical hyperscaler per-GPU-hour costs, particularly when workloads fully utilize bare-metal performance gains.

## When it fits

- **Large-scale LLM and foundation model training:** Teams training models with billions of parameters benefit from direct bare-metal access and high-bandwidth InfiniBand clusters.
- **Multi-GPU distributed training:** InfiniBand connectivity and up to 8,176-node clusters support synchronous, communication-intensive workloads.
- **Research institutions and AI labs:** Predictable bare-metal performance and academic-friendly terms attract non-commercial AI research.
- **Startups scaling from prototype to production:** On-demand flexibility with no contracts bridges the gap between development and reserved capacity.
- **Low-latency, bandwidth-sensitive training:** Direct hardware access eliminates virtualization latency; Quantum-2 InfiniBand minimizes cluster communication overhead.

## When it doesn't

Voltage Park is not a good fit for general-purpose cloud workloads, microservices, or applications requiring managed Kubernetes, databases, or storage. It also lacks the geographic redundancy and global region coverage of AWS, GCP, or Azure, limiting multi-region failover and compliance scenarios.
