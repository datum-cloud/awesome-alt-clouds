## What makes E2E Cloud different

E2E Cloud positions itself as India's premier GPU cloud provider, focusing specifically on AI and machine learning workloads. Their key differentiator is rapid deployment speed—they claim 10-second GPU provisioning with no queues or quotas, compared to typical cloud providers that may have lengthy wait times for GPU resources. The platform offers immediate access to the latest NVIDIA GPUs including B200, H200, and H100 instances with high-speed InfiniBand networking.

The company emphasizes cost savings of up to 70% versus hyperscalers while maintaining enterprise-grade security certifications including SOC2, ISO 27001, ISO 27017, ISO 27018, and PCI DSS compliance. They provide elastic scaling from 10 to 1000+ GPUs in 90 seconds and include 1 GBps distributed storage per GPU optimized for AI workloads.

## Pricing model

GPU instances start at $1.80/hour for NVIDIA H100 (80GB SXM with 26 vCPU, 250GB RAM), $2.20/hour for H200 (141GB SXM with 30 vCPU, 375GB RAM), and $4.90/hour for B200 (192GB HBM3e with 26 vCPU, 250GB RAM). All configurations include 3.2 Tbps InfiniBand networking. Pricing is hourly with volume discounts available for commitments of hundreds of units for 3+ months. The pricing model targets significant cost savings compared to major cloud providers while offering immediate availability without quotas.

## When it fits

• AI and machine learning teams needing immediate GPU access without waiting in queues
• Organizations requiring cost-effective alternatives to AWS/Azure/GCP for GPU workloads
• Companies needing to scale GPU resources rapidly from 10 to 1000+ instances
• Enterprises requiring SOC2 and ISO-certified infrastructure for compliance
• Teams deploying large language models and other compute-intensive AI applications

## When it doesn't

E2E Cloud appears primarily focused on the Indian market and GPU-centric workloads, so it may not be suitable for global enterprises needing worldwide data center presence or traditional non-AI compute workloads that don't require specialized GPU infrastructure.

## Inclusion criteria

E2E Cloud meets all three inclusion criteria: transparent pricing is displayed on their website with specific hourly rates for GPU instances, they offer self-service signup capabilities, and they provide a 99.95% uptime SLA guarantee as stated on their homepage.
