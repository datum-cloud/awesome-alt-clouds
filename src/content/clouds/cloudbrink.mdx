## What makes Cloudbrink different

Cloudbrink is a network and security-focused cloud provider that prioritizes performance and zero-trust architecture over traditional VPN-based access models. Rather than treating remote access as a legacy infrastructure problem, Cloudbrink has engineered its platform around edge-native networking and automated moving target defense—shifting security from perimeter-based models to per-user, per-connection enforcement.

The platform is designed as a personal SASE (Secure Access Service Edge) solution, meaning each user gets a dedicated security stack rather than shared enterprise infrastructure. This architectural choice delivers measurably lower latency and packet loss for video conferencing, VDI, and real-time applications compared to traditional VPN or centralized SASE offerings. The system integrates across AWS, Azure, and third-party cloud providers, enabling true multi-cloud networking without forcing workload consolidation.

Cloudbrink's moving target defense mechanism automatically obscures user endpoints and varies security policies in real time, reducing the attack surface by eliminating static IP associations and predictable access patterns—a significant departure from conventional zero-trust approaches that rely on fixed policy enforcement.

## Pricing model

Cloudbrink uses a subscription-based pricing model, with transparent tiering available through both direct sales and AWS Marketplace. While specific per-user or per-gigabyte pricing is not publicly listed on their website, the company positions itself as a cost-effective alternative to traditional enterprise VPN licensing and supports self-service provisioning for qualification and demo signup.

The model stands out for transparency in offering trial access and demo environments without hidden deployment fees, and availability through AWS Marketplace reduces procurement friction for AWS-native organizations.

## When it fits

- **Hybrid and remote-first workforces** needing fast, secure access to cloud and on-premises resources without VPN performance penalties
- **Unified Communications and Video** deployments (Teams, Zoom, WebEx) where latency and packet loss directly impact user experience
- **VDI and desktop-as-a-service** environments requiring high-performance secure tunnels
- **Multi-cloud organizations** managing workloads across AWS, Azure, and private infrastructure without a single cloud commitment
- **Security-conscious enterprises** adopting zero-trust models and seeking automated moving-target defense beyond static policy engines

## When it doesn't

Cloudbrink is not a general-purpose compute or storage cloud provider. Organizations seeking IaaS VMs, managed databases, or serverless functions should use AWS, Azure, or GCP instead. It is also network-layer software, not a replacement for application-layer security or DLP controls.
