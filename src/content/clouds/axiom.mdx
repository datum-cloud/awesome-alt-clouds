## What makes Axiom different

Axiom positions itself as an observability platform built specifically for modern, high-cardinality data without the traditional sampling limitations. Unlike conventional logging and monitoring solutions that require data sampling to control costs, Axiom's architecture enables organizations to ingest and retain all their observability data affordably for extended periods—what they call "volumetric logging."

The platform unifies logs, traces, and metrics in a single interface, with particular focus on AI engineering workflows. Axiom provides specialized tooling for AI workflow tracing, prompt evaluation, and cost/latency tracking across AI providers, addressing the emerging need for AI observability as organizations deploy more AI-powered applications.

## Pricing model

Axiom uses a usage-based pricing model centered around data ingestion and retention. The platform emphasizes cost-effectiveness for high-volume data scenarios, with customers reporting significant savings compared to traditional observability tools. One customer testimonial specifically mentions being able to "affordably see all my logs for a year," suggesting competitive long-term retention pricing. Specific pricing tiers and numbers are available through their pricing comparison page, though exact figures were not visible in the provided content.

## When it fits

- Organizations generating high-cardinality observability data that need complete visibility without sampling
- AI/ML teams requiring specialized tracing and evaluation capabilities for AI workflows
- Development teams using modern infrastructure (serverless, edge computing, microservices)
- Companies seeking unified observability (logs, traces, metrics) in a single platform
- Teams with OpenTelemetry-based instrumentation looking for native integration

## When it doesn't

Axiom may not be ideal for organizations with simple monitoring needs or those already heavily invested in traditional APM solutions with established workflows.
