## What makes Amplitude different

Amplitude is a product analytics and digital intelligence platform designed specifically to help product, engineering, and marketing teams understand user behavior and build data-driven products. Unlike general-purpose cloud data warehouses, Amplitude provides a specialized analytics stack with pre-built features for tracking user journeys, running experiments, and acting on behavioral insights—without requiring heavy SQL expertise.

The platform distinguishes itself through its AI-native approach, including AI Agents that can sense, decide, and act autonomously on product data, and an AI Visibility feature that shows how products appear in AI search results. This represents a shift beyond traditional analytics dashboards toward autonomous decision-making systems powered by behavioral data. Amplitude also emphasizes data governance and unified activation, allowing teams to centralize insights and feed them directly into product experiments and feature management workflows.

## Pricing model

Amplitude uses a usage-based pricing model tied to tracked events. The platform offers free plans for startups and a self-service pay-as-you-go tier for growing teams. Exact per-event pricing is not published on the website and requires contacting sales, though the model is designed to be transparent and scale with product usage rather than fixed annual commitments. This contrasts with some competitors that charge per user or per data retention period. Enterprise customers can negotiate custom commitments.

## When it fits

- **Product teams** building mobile or web applications that need detailed user journey analytics and funnel tracking
- **Experimentation-focused teams** running A/B tests, feature flags, and cohort-based product experiments at scale
- **Early-stage startups** taking advantage of free analytics tools and simple event tracking without complex data infrastructure
- **Behavior-driven businesses** (e-commerce, SaaS, media) that benefit from session replay, heatmaps, and real-time engagement insights
- **Teams moving fast** who need pre-integrated feature management and activation without custom ETL pipelines

## When it doesn't

Amplitude is not a general-purpose data warehouse or compute platform. It is poorly suited for data teams needing SQL-based analytics, machine learning model serving, or long-term data archival at scale. Organizations requiring on-premises deployment or complete data residency control should evaluate alternatives.
