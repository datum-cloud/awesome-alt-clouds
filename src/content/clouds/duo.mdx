## What makes Duo different

Duo Security, now part of Cisco, distinguishes itself by placing the device and the user context at the center of identity management, rather than relying solely on perimeter-based network controls. Its core differentiator is "Device Trust," which verifies the health and compliance of the endpoint before granting access. This approach allows Duo to enforce granular, risk-based policies—such as blocking access from unpatched devices or unusual locations—without requiring complex infrastructure changes.

Unlike traditional IAM solutions that often treat MFA as an afterthought or a separate bolt-on, Duo integrates authentication, authorization, and device posture into a single, cohesive platform. This integration extends to "Agentic IAM," a newer capability designed to secure AI agents and automated workflows in addition to human users, ensuring that non-human identities are also subject to zero-trust principles. This makes Duo particularly effective for organizations transitioning to hybrid or remote work models where network boundaries are blurred.

## Pricing model

Duo operates on a subscription-based model, typically sold per user per month. While specific public price lists are not available on the general homepage, enterprise pricing is generally structured around tiered feature sets (Standard, Enterprise, and Advanced editions).

Key pricing differentiators include:

- **Bundled Features:** Higher tiers bundle MFA, SSO, and Device Trust, reducing the need for separate vendor contracts.
- **Volume Discounts:** Significant discounts are available for large-scale deployments, common in enterprise and government sectors.
- **No Hidden Infrastructure Costs:** As a SaaS identity provider, Duo does not charge for compute or storage, unlike cloud infrastructure providers.

## When it fits

- **Hybrid/Remote Workforces:** Organizations needing consistent security policies across on-premises and cloud applications.
- **High-Security Compliance:** Industries like healthcare, finance, and government requiring strict audit trails and phishing-resistant authentication.
- **Identity Consolidation:** Teams looking to replace disparate MFA and SSO tools with a unified platform.
- **Agent Security:** Enterprises deploying autonomous AI agents that require secure, trusted identity management.

## When it doesn't

- **Compute/Storage Needs:** Duo does not provide infrastructure services like VMs, databases, or object storage.
- **Simple Internal Tools:** Small teams with minimal security requirements may find Duo’s comprehensive feature set overkill compared to simpler, cheaper MFA solutions.
