## What makes Megaport different

Megaport specializes in **network interconnection** rather than compute or storage. Its core product is software-defined networking that lets organizations provision virtual connections between cloud providers, data centers, and networks on-demand—without long-term contracts or hardware installation delays.

Unlike hyperscalers that compete on compute power and storage capacity, Megaport solves the "last mile" problem: how to connect infrastructure efficiently. It acts as a neutral intermediary, supporting direct integrations with AWS Direct Connect, Azure ExpressRoute, Google Cloud Partner Interconnect, Oracle FastConnect, and others. This multi-cloud positioning is particularly valuable for organizations avoiding vendor lock-in or operating across multiple cloud environments.

The platform emphasizes self-service provisioning through its portal and API, enabling network engineers to spin up virtual connections in minutes rather than weeks.

## Pricing model

Megaport operates on a **usage-based, pay-as-you-go model**. Specific pricing tiers are not disclosed publicly on their main website; pricing varies by port speed (1 Gbps, 10 Gbps, 100 Gbps options) and connection type (Virtual Cross Connect, Megaport Port, etc.). Organizations are directed to contact sales or access the pricing page at [megaport.com/pricing](https://www.megaport.com/pricing/) for quotes.

This approach differs from typical cloud pricing in that costs are tied to **bandwidth provisioning and connectivity duration** rather than compute instance hours. There are no long-term contract requirements—connections can be provisioned and deprovisioned on demand, making it cost-effective for temporary cross-cloud migrations or burst traffic scenarios.

## When it fits

- **Multi-cloud architectures**: Organizations running workloads across AWS, Azure, GCP, and Oracle simultaneously, needing low-latency interconnection without data egress penalties.
- **Hybrid cloud deployments**: On-premises data centers connecting to multiple cloud providers with predictable, low-latency paths.
- **Data center migrations**: Temporary high-bandwidth connections between legacy infrastructure and cloud environments during transition periods.
- **Financial services and real-time trading**: Industries requiring ultra-low-latency, dedicated network paths between exchanges, data centers, and cloud providers.
- **Content delivery and edge networks**: Connecting distributed data centers or edge locations to centralized cloud platforms via the Megaport ecosystem.

## When it doesn't

Megaport is **not a compute or storage provider**—it does not replace AWS EC2, Azure VMs, or cloud databases. Organizations seeking managed compute, databases, or object storage at hyperscaler scale should use AWS, Azure, or GCP directly. Small teams with single-cloud strategies may find Megaport's complexity and cost unnecessary.
