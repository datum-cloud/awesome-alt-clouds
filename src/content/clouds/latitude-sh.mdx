## What makes Latitude.sh different

Latitude.sh bridges bare metal infrastructure with cloud-native agility. Unlike hyperscalers that virtualize everything, Latitude operates its own hardware and autonomous network systems across 25 global regions, enabling servers to deploy in under 5 seconds without the abstraction layer overhead. This appeals to workloads that demand raw compute power—AI/ML training, blockchain validators, game servers—while retaining cloud conveniences like API-driven provisioning, Terraform support, and pay-as-you-go billing.

The platform specifically targets Web3 and AI companies seeking geographic flexibility without vendor lock-in. Its Cloud Gateway service privately connects Latitude infrastructure to AWS, GCP, and Azure, positioning it as a cost-efficient compute layer alongside hyperscalers rather than a replacement. Customers like Boundless (ZK-proving) and X Labs (Solana infrastructure) have achieved 100% uptime in production, signaling reliability for mission-critical workloads.

## Pricing model

Latitude.sh uses hourly billing for bare metal servers, GPUs, and virtual machines. Specific per-unit pricing is not published on the homepage; customers must navigate to the pricing page or contact sales for quotes. This approach is typical for bare metal providers, where hardware configurations (CPU count, RAM, GPU type) and regional availability drive costs. The platform's transparency score is strong—hourly pricing is clearly advertised—but granular rate cards are not immediately accessible, likely reflecting the customizable nature of bare metal configurations.

## When it fits

- **AI/ML workloads**: GPU clusters pre-configured with ML tools and bare metal for training jobs requiring low latency and high throughput.
- **Blockchain infrastructure**: Solana validators, Ethereum nodes, and ZK-proving systems needing deterministic performance and geographic distribution.
- **High-performance gaming**: Multiplayer game servers benefiting from global edge locations and DDoS mitigation.
- **Cloud migration from Equinix Metal**: Purpose-built migration path for customers outgrowing managed bare metal.
- **Multi-cloud strategies**: Organizations leveraging Cloud Gateway to burst compute across Latitude and hyperscalers while optimizing costs.

## When it doesn't

Latitude is not ideal for stateless web applications, SaaS with variable load, or teams without infrastructure expertise. Bare metal requires manual OS patching and hardware lifecycle management; serverless or fully managed alternatives (Lambda, Cloud Run) are better for event-driven workloads. High-touch enterprise procurement may find the self-service model limiting.
