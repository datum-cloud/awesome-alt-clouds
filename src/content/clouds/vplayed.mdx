## What makes VPlayed different

VPlayed departs from the typical hyperscaler model by selling a licensable, white-label OTT software stack rather than metered cloud infrastructure. Customers get source-code access and can host the platform on their own cloud account or on-premise servers, rather than being locked into VPlayed's own infrastructure. This "host anywhere" architecture means the product is really a deployable video streaming application layer — covering live/VOD delivery, DRM, transcoding, ad insertion, and a CMS — that sits on top of whatever compute and CDN the customer chooses.

The core differentiator is the commercial model: a one-time licensing cost instead of recurring per-stream or per-GB fees, combined with no revenue sharing on monetization. This is aimed at media brands and content creators who want to run their own branded Netflix/YouTube-style service without giving a cut of subscription or ad revenue to the platform vendor, and without being bound to usage-based cloud video pricing that scales unpredictably with viewership.

It also bundles AI features (recommendations, auto-metadata, auto-subtitles) and 200+ third-party integrations directly into the product, positioning it as a full OTT business toolkit rather than raw infrastructure — closer to a SaaS/PaaS hybrid for the media vertical than a general-purpose cloud.

## Pricing model

VPlayed's stated model is a one-time payment for the platform license, which the company claims can save up to 75% versus ongoing monthly/annual subscription costs typical of competing OTT SaaS products. Exact dollar figures are not published on the homepage; pricing appears to be quote-based depending on feature set, hosting choice (cloud vs on-premise), and customization scope. What stands out versus typical cloud pricing is the absence of recurring infrastructure-style billing (no per-GB egress, no per-stream fees, no revenue share) — customers pay once and then run the platform on infrastructure of their choosing.

## When it fits

- Media brands or content creators who want a fully branded OTT/streaming app without building one from scratch
- Businesses that want to avoid recurring SaaS fees or revenue-sharing arrangements with streaming vendors
- Teams needing flexible hosting (cloud or on-premise) rather than being locked to one provider's infrastructure
- Organizations needing live streaming, VOD, short-form video, and linear/Cloud TV under one unified CMS
- Companies wanting fast time-to-market (claimed 2-day launch) for a monetized video service

## When it doesn't

- Teams needing raw, general-purpose cloud infrastructure (compute, storage, databases) outside the video/media use case
- Organizations wanting fully managed, zero-ops hosting without taking on deployment and infrastructure responsibility themselves
