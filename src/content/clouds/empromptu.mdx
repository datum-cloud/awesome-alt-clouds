## What makes Empromptu different

Empromptu takes a fundamentally different approach to enterprise AI by building custom applications that simultaneously train proprietary models from real usage data. Instead of offering generic cloud compute or pre-built AI services, they develop bespoke AI applications for enterprises and use every interaction, correction, and edge case as training data for custom models. This creates a compounding intelligence effect where the AI gets better with each use.

The platform promises enterprise-grade deployments in 30 days without requiring dedicated AI engineers, handling everything from initial application development to ongoing model refinement. They emphasize compliance from day one with SOC 2 and HIPAA certifications, positioning themselves as a turnkey solution for enterprises that need production-ready AI without the traditional 6-12 month development cycles.

## Pricing model

Empromptu uses a subscription-based pricing model but does not publish specific pricing tiers on their website. They emphasize a consultative approach with strategy sessions and custom enterprise deployments, suggesting pricing is likely tailored to individual use cases and enterprise requirements rather than standardized tiers.

## When it fits

• Enterprises needing custom AI applications with compliance requirements (SOC 2, HIPAA)
• Companies wanting AI that improves automatically from real-world usage patterns
• Organizations without dedicated AI engineering teams but requiring production-ready solutions
• Businesses in regulated industries like healthcare, finance, or legal services
• Companies that need AI integrated into existing workflows without platform rewrites

## When it doesn't
