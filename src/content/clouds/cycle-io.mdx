## What makes Cycle.io different

Cycle.io positions itself as a Kubernetes alternative that eliminates operational overhead while maintaining enterprise-grade capabilities. The platform runs on customer-owned infrastructure across any provider—from bare metal to public cloud—while delivering a unified control plane that automates networking, OS patching, and state management.

The key differentiator is infrastructure ownership combined with platform simplicity. Unlike hyperscaler PaaS offerings, customers retain complete control over where their data lives and how applications deploy, with no vendor lock-in. Cycle abstracts away the complexity of container orchestration through CycleOS, a minimal, atomic operating system that provides self-healing nodes and rolling updates.

## Pricing model

Cycle operates on a subscription-based pricing model, though specific pricing tiers are not publicly disclosed on their website. The platform charges for the control plane service while customers pay their chosen infrastructure providers directly for compute and storage resources. This approach allows teams to optimize infrastructure costs while paying a predictable fee for the orchestration layer.

## When it fits

• Teams migrating from complex Kubernetes setups seeking operational simplicity
• Organizations requiring strict data sovereignty and infrastructure ownership
• SaaS and PaaS builders needing multi-tenant isolation primitives
• Companies running hybrid environments across multiple cloud providers
• Development teams wanting to focus on building rather than DevOps maintenance

## When it doesn't

Cycle may not suit teams comfortable with Kubernetes complexity or those requiring specific K8s ecosystem tools. Organizations needing serverless-first architectures might find hyperscaler offerings more suitable.

## Inclusion criteria

Cycle.io meets all three inclusion criteria: the platform offers self-service signup, maintains transparent communication about their service model, and provides customer testimonials indicating reliable service delivery with metrics like 150+ million hourly requests processed.
