## What makes Firebolt different

Firebolt is purpose-built for analytical workloads that demand both speed and scale. Unlike general-purpose cloud data warehouses, Firebolt's architecture optimizes for low-latency query performance on large datasets through GPU-accelerated indexing and a columnar storage engine. The platform separates compute from storage, allowing independent scaling of analytics engines and data repositories—a design choice that reduces costs when running mixed or bursty workloads.

The product targets engineers and analytics teams that need millisecond-level query response times for interactive dashboards, real-time reporting, and exploratory data analysis. Firebolt competes on performance benchmarks rather than ecosystem breadth, positioning itself as faster than Snowflake, Redshift, and BigQuery for typical OLAP queries.

Notably, Firebolt offers **Firebolt Core**, a free, self-hosted analytical query engine that runs on any infrastructure—a hybrid play that lets customers avoid cloud vendor lock-in while maintaining the option to scale into managed Firebolt Cloud.

## Pricing model

Firebolt uses a usage-based pricing model tied to compute consumption. The platform charges for **compute per second** during query execution, with pricing varying by engine size and region. Storage is billed separately at a per-GB-per-month rate.

A free tier provides access to Firebolt's managed cloud service with limited resources, and Firebolt Core remains perpetually free for self-hosted deployments. This hybrid model differentiates Firebolt from pure SaaS competitors—teams can prototype for free, scale to cloud when needed, and retain the option to run workloads on-premises. Pricing details are available on the [Pricing page](https://www.firebolt.io/pricing).

## When it fits

- **Real-time dashboards & BI**: Sub-second query latency for interactive analytics tools and live reporting dashboards.
- **High-cardinality data exploration**: Filtering, aggregating, and drilling into datasets with millions of unique values.
- **Variable or bursty workloads**: On-demand compute scaling without paying for idle resources; separating compute from storage avoids overprovisioning.
- **Multi-tenancy at scale**: Isolating analytic workloads across customers with guaranteed performance isolation.
- **Cost-sensitive analytics**: Lower total cost of ownership compared to always-on warehouse solutions for batch or time-shifted queries.

## When it doesn't

Firebolt is not a good fit for transactional OLTP workloads, data lakes requiring unstructured data support, or teams deeply invested in Hadoop/Spark ecosystems. Organizations requiring extensive ETL orchestration tooling may need to integrate Firebolt with external workflow platforms like dbt or Apache Airflow.
