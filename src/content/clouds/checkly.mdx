## What makes Checkly different

Checkly positions itself as the "active reliability layer" purpose-built for developers and AI agents. Its core differentiation lies in **Monitoring as Code**—a JavaScript/TypeScript-first approach via the Checkly CLI where tests, monitors, and alerts are defined as code and version-controlled like application infrastructure. This contrasts with point-and-click UI-driven platforms like Pingdom or even Datadog's UI-heavy experience.

The platform is deeply integrated with **Playwright**, the headless browser automation framework, enabling synthetic checks that simulate real user flows (login, checkout, transactions) at scale across 20+ global locations. Unlike traditional synthetic monitors that ping endpoints, Checkly can replicate complex browser interactions and capture full performance waterfall data, response times, and visual regression detection.

A second differentiator is native **AI agent integration**. The platform explicitly supports Claude, Cursor, Windsurf, and OpenAI, allowing AI assistants to author and deploy monitors via natural language prompts (e.g., "Monitor our checkout flow from 4 global regions"). This makes it a natural fit for AI-driven development workflows emerging in 2024–2025.

## Pricing model

Checkly does not publicly disclose per-unit pricing on its website. The pricing page directs users to request a demo or sign up for a free tier. However, the model appears **subscription-based**, tiered by check frequency, number of checks, and global regions selected. The free tier allows getting started without a credit card, and the platform bills monthly.

The approach is more developer-friendly than hyperscaler pricing: costs scale with reliability requirements (how often you check, how many locations) rather than compute consumed, making budgeting predictable for teams that run the same monitors continuously.

## When it fits

- **API and user flow testing in CI/CD pipelines** – Playwright checks catch regressions before production; integrates with Terraform/Pulumi for IaC workflows.
- **Global availability monitoring** – Teams needing uptime visibility from 20+ regions (Americas, Europe, Asia-Pacific) without managing probe infrastructure.
- **AI-driven development teams** – Workflows where Claude or other LLMs compose and deploy monitors reduce toil.
- **Incident response and root cause analysis** – Rocky AI and distributed tracing pinpoint failures across the stack; deep Slack, PagerDuty, and OpsGenie integrations.
- **Status page automation** – Public status page updates automatically reflect real-time synthetic check results.

## When it doesn't

- **Low-cost, simple uptime pings** – Services like Uptimerobot or Pingdom offer cheaper, simpler endpoint monitoring if you don't need browser automation or global scale.
- **Deep infrastructure monitoring** – Checkly is application/endpoint-focused; teams needing VM-level metrics, custom instrumentation, or OTEL collectors at scale may prefer Datadog or New Relic.
