## What makes Seeweb different

Seeweb positions itself as a European alternative to global hyperscalers with a strong sustainability mandate. All data centers run on 100% renewable energy—a commitment baked into the company's founding principles rather than a later sustainability initiative. The provider operates four regional data centers (Milan, Frosinone, Sofia, Lugano) with a focus on Italian and Central European markets, giving it geographic proximity and local compliance expertise.

Unlike AWS, GCP, or Azure, Seeweb emphasizes direct technical partnerships with system integrators and engineers. The platform supports both VMware and Proxmox for Virtual Private Cloud, offering flexibility in hypervisor choice. GPU cloud services are explicitly designed for AI/ML workloads, reflecting growing demand in that segment. All services come with transparent SLA guarantees (up to 99.99% uptime) and predictive support options, positioning reliability as a competitive advantage.

## Pricing model

Seeweb does not publicly list detailed per-unit pricing on their homepage. However, they advertise a flexible, **mixed model** combining hourly billing for on-demand resources (Cloud Server, GPU) and subscription tiers for managed services (Kubernetes, support plans). Support is offered in three tiers: Basic, Global, and Proactive. The partner program includes customizable credit policies, suggesting enterprise negotiation is available.

This approach contrasts with hyperscaler consumption-based metering; Seeweb emphasizes predictability and direct relationships over algorithmic pricing complexity.

## When it fits

- **European compliance-heavy workloads** requiring GDPR-aligned infrastructure and local data residency (financial services, healthcare, public sector).
- **AI/ML development** projects needing GPU acceleration without multi-region lock-in or long-term commitment.
- **Sustainability-conscious organizations** prioritizing 100% renewable energy as a core procurement criterion.
- **VMware or Proxmox shops** seeking managed cloud without forced migration to proprietary orchestration.
- **Mid-market and enterprise** customers valuing dedicated technical support and custom infrastructure design over self-service scaling.

## When it doesn't

- **Global multi-region failover** requirements; Seeweb's four regions are concentrated in Europe/Southern Europe.
- **Serverless or managed database services** at AWS/Azure parity; Seeweb's focus is infrastructure and containers, not platform-as-a-service depth.

## Inclusion criteria

✅ **Transparent pricing** — Cloud Server and GPU offerings support self-service configuration; pricing is quoted on-demand.

✅ **Self-service signup** — Self-service provisioning available for Cloud Server and Virtual Private Cloud products.

✅ **Public SLA / Status page** — Seeweb publishes SLA terms (up to 99.99% uptime) at [https://www.seeweb.it/data-center/data-center-sla](https://www.seeweb.it/data-center/data-center-sla).
