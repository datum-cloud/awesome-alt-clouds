## What makes Macrometa different

Macrometa positions itself as a specialized edge delivery and developer platform rather than a general-purpose cloud provider. Its core differentiator is the **PhotonIQ suite**—a set of AI-driven services that optimize web performance at the edge without requiring code changes. Unlike hyperscalers that offer broad compute, storage, and networking primitives, Macrometa targets specific business outcomes: improving Core Web Vitals, reducing bounce rates, and accelerating organic search rankings.

The platform leverages a globally distributed edge network to serve content closer to end users, reducing latency for eCommerce, SaaS, and media delivery use cases. This architecture is particularly useful for businesses seeking plug-and-play performance gains rather than infrastructure-level control.

## Pricing model

Macrometa uses a usage-based pricing model tied to traffic volume and edge services consumed. Specific per-unit pricing is not publicly detailed on their homepage; instead, they emphasize a self-service onboarding flow and direct sales consultation for custom quotes. This approach is typical for edge platforms where cost depends heavily on request volume, geographic distribution, and feature utilization.

The model stands apart from hyperscaler pricing by focusing on **business outcome metrics** (conversion rate lift, SEO improvement) rather than raw compute hours or bandwidth. Customers are encouraged to contact sales for transparent, personalized pricing.

## When it fits

- **eCommerce and retail sites** needing sub-second page load times and improved search visibility without code refactoring
- **High-traffic digital properties** requiring dynamic content optimization and SEO for AI crawlers
- **Media and streaming platforms** delivering content globally with minimal latency
- **SaaS applications** serving distributed user bases where edge caching and performance directly impact user retention
- **Businesses prioritizing SEO and organic traffic** growth over paid search spend

## When it doesn't

Macrometa is not a replacement for general-purpose cloud infrastructure (compute, databases, storage). It's a complementary service best suited as an edge layer in front of existing backends, not as a standalone platform for building applications from scratch.
