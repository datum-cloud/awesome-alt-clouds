## What makes Attio different

Attio distinguishes itself by reimagining the CRM not just as a database, but as an "intelligent system that never sleeps." Unlike traditional CRMs that require manual data entry and rigid pipeline management, Attio leverages **Universal Context™** to aggregate every signal from every customer interaction into a single, live layer. This architecture allows sales, success, and finance teams to operate on the same real-time picture of the customer without siloed data.

The platform’s core differentiator is its **Revenue Agents**. These are AI-driven workflows that automatically pick up leads, catch renewal signals, and draft communications. By embedding AI directly into the operational workflow rather than treating it as an add-on, Attio reduces administrative overhead and ensures no deal slips through the cracks. The infrastructure is built to handle millions of records with sub-50ms latency, ensuring that the "continuous context" remains instant and actionable.

Furthermore, Attio prioritizes flexibility in data structure. Users can design their own workflows and data schemas, making it adaptable to complex sales motions that standard CRMs often struggle to accommodate. This developer-friendly approach extends to its API and integration capabilities, allowing teams to connect their existing tech stack seamlessly while maintaining data integrity.

## Pricing model

Attio operates on a **subscription-based** pricing model. While specific per-seat costs are not publicly listed in the provided source text, the company offers a transparent pricing page where users can select tiers based on their team size and feature requirements. This model typically includes access to core CRM features, AI agents, and reporting tools within each tier.

The standout aspect of Attio's pricing is its value alignment with revenue generation. Unlike hyperscaler cloud providers that charge for compute and storage, Attio charges for productivity and intelligence. The inclusion of AI agents and automated workflows in standard tiers means customers pay for outcomes (closed deals, saved time) rather than raw infrastructure resources. This makes cost predictability straightforward for sales organizations, with no hidden fees for data volume or API calls beyond the subscription limits.

## When it fits

- **High-velocity sales teams** that need automated lead capture and follow-up without manual data entry.
- **Organizations seeking a modern CRM alternative** to Salesforce or HubSpot, prioritizing UI/UX and flexibility over legacy features.
- **Teams requiring deep data integration** where "Universal Context" can unify signals from email, calls, and third-party apps.
- **Startups and scale-ups** looking for an enterprise-ready security posture (ISO 27001, GDPR, CCPA) out of the box without complex compliance setups.
- **Revenue operations teams** that need real-time forecasting and cohort analysis without building custom dashboards.

## When it doesn't

- **Workloads requiring raw infrastructure control:** Attio is a SaaS application, not an IaaS provider, so it cannot host compute instances, databases, or networking resources.
- **Highly regulated industries with strict data residency requirements:** While Attio complies with GDPR and CCPA, specific local data sovereignty laws may require on-premise or region-specific cloud infrastructure that Attio does not currently offer.
