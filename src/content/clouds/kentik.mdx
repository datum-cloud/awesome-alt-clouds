## What makes Kentik different

Kentik specializes in network observability across hybrid and multi-cloud environments—a focus distinctly narrower than hyperscaler platforms. Rather than offering compute, storage, and databases, Kentik provides deep visibility into network traffic flows, performance bottlenecks, and security threats across on-premises data centers, cloud providers (AWS, GCP, Azure, IBM Cloud), and the broader internet.

The platform is purpose-built for network teams, cloud infrastructure engineers, and traffic engineers who need to understand *why* networks behave the way they do. Kentik's DDoS detection capability is particularly noted as "the most accurate in the market," distinguishing it from generic monitoring solutions. The company's founding team includes veterans who helped build the global internet backbone, informing a product architecture that prioritizes real-time flow data analysis and context over agent-based telemetry alone.

## Pricing model

Kentik uses a usage-based pricing model, though exact per-unit costs are not publicly listed on their website. Pricing scales with the volume of network traffic analyzed and data retention periods. The platform typically requires direct contact with sales for custom quotes based on organization size and traffic patterns. This approach contrasts with hyperscaler monitoring services, which often bundle observability into broader service fees; Kentik's standalone model may be more cost-effective for organizations primarily concerned with network visibility rather than compute infrastructure.

## When it fits

- **Multi-cloud and hybrid network operations**: Organizations running workloads across AWS, GCP, Azure, and on-premises data centers that need unified visibility across all network paths
- **Cloud cost optimization**: Teams looking to reduce egress charges, optimize VPC peering vs. Transit Gateway usage, and right-size network architecture
- **DDoS detection and mitigation**: Security teams that need rapid, accurate distinction between legitimate traffic spikes and malicious attacks
- **Network capacity planning**: Infrastructure teams forecasting utilization trends and justifying hardware refresh or link upgrades
- **Incident response**: NOC and support teams reducing mean time to detection (MTTD) and resolution (MTTR) for network-related outages

## When it doesn't

Kentik is not a replacement for general-purpose cloud platforms or application performance monitoring tools. It is poorly suited for teams needing compute provisioning, database hosting, or serverless capabilities—use AWS, GCP, or Azure for those workloads. Organizations with primarily internal, non-cloud networks may find the multi-cloud feature set unnecessary.
