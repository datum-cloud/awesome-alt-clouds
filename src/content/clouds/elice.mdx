## What makes Elice different

Elice positions itself as an "AI full-stack" company rather than a pure infrastructure provider — it bundles GPU cloud compute (ECI, Elice AI Cloud) with AI education and workforce-training products (Elice LXP, Elice Academy) and enterprise generative AI tools (Helpy Chat). This is a distinct go-to-market from hyperscalers: customers can procure GPU infrastructure, train staff on AI/ML skills, and deploy generative AI applications from a single vendor relationship.

On the infrastructure side, Elice has publicized work with NVIDIA B200 and B300 GPU clusters, including a reported 128-GPU B200 cluster validation with LG AI Research and a proof-of-concept deployment for NC AI. It also recently announced Korea's first warm-water cooled AI data center built on its "AI PMDC" (modular data center) product, which it claims can be stood up in roughly three months — a notably fast deployment model compared to traditional hyperscale data center build-outs.

Elice's customer base skews toward Korean enterprises, universities, and research labs (e.g., Korea University's AGI Lab, Hyundai Motor Group, SPC, Clio Cosmetics), as well as some international academic partners (University of Minnesota, Keio University), suggesting a regional specialization in Korea/APAC with growing research-sector traction.

## Pricing model

Elice's site references a dedicated Pricing page for its cloud and platform products, indicating usage-based billing for compute resources (consistent with GPU cloud norms), though specific per-GPU-hour or per-seat figures were not available in the crawled content. Enterprise offerings like AI PMDC and large GPU cluster deployments (B200/B300) appear to be quoted/contracted rather than self-serve metered, typical for data-center-scale engagements. What stands out is the bundling potential — infrastructure, training, and AI software can be priced and packaged together for enterprise AX (AI transformation) initiatives, rather than compute being sold in isolation.

## When it fits

- Korean or APAC-based enterprises and institutions pursuing an "AI transformation" (AX) initiative that need compute, tooling, and staff training together
- Research labs and universities running large-scale LLM or RL experiments needing access to modern GPU clusters (B200/B300-class hardware)
- Organizations wanting a turnkey, fast-to-deploy private AI data center (AI PMDC) rather than building one from scratch
- Enterprises wanting an internal generative AI assistant (Helpy Chat) alongside infrastructure
- Teams needing combined e-learning/competency assessment plus cloud GPU access under one vendor

## When it doesn't

- Teams needing broad multi-region global infrastructure or presence outside Korea/APAC should look elsewhere, as Elice's disclosed regions are Korea-centric.
- Workloads requiring simple, fully self-serve, transparent per-hour GPU pricing without enterprise sales engagement may find Elice's model less frictionless than pure-play GPU clouds.
