## What makes Elice different

Elice positions itself as an "AI full-stack" company rather than a pure infrastructure provider: it bundles GPU cloud compute (ECI, Elice AI Cloud) with learning and talent-development tools (Elice LXP, Elice Test) and generative AI products (Helpy Chat, Helpy Vision). This is a distinct go-to-market from hyperscalers — Elice sells AI transformation (AX) as an outcome, combining infrastructure, workforce training, and applied AI products in one contract, targeting enterprises and universities that need to both build AI capability and train people to use it.

On the infrastructure side, Elice has built out dedicated GPU data center capacity, including a modular AI data center product (AI PMDC) that it claims can be stood up in three months, and has publicized deployments of NVIDIA B200 and B300 GPU clusters (including a warm-water cooled B300 data center) for customers such as LG AI Research and NC AI. This suggests a focus on large-scale GPU cluster provisioning for LLM training and inference workloads rather than general-purpose compute.

The company's customer base skews heavily toward Korean enterprises, government-backed research institutions, and universities (e.g., Korea University's AGI Lab, University of Minnesota), suggesting strength in GPU cluster procurement and AI education/training programs for regulated or research-oriented organizations, alongside more conventional cloud GPU rental.

## Pricing model

Elice lists a dedicated Pricing page (linked from its site footer), and billing for its AI cloud/GPU infrastructure is usage-based. No specific per-GPU-hour or tiered dollar figures were available in the page content retrieved for this profile — consult elice.io's Pricing page directly for current GPU instance rates, as exact figures could not be confirmed from the crawled content.

What stands out versus typical hyperscaler pricing is the bundling model: customers can pair raw GPU compute (ECI) with packaged AI transformation services (training content, assessment tools, generative AI apps) rather than purchasing compute as a standalone commodity.

## When it fits

- Enterprises running large-scale LLM training or fine-tuning on high-end GPUs (B200/B300 clusters)
- Organizations pursuing a combined "AI transformation" initiative that needs both infrastructure and workforce upskilling
- Universities and research labs needing GPU cloud access for LLM/RL research
- Companies wanting to deploy generative AI chat/vision tools (Helpy Chat/Vision) alongside underlying compute
- Organizations primarily operating in or serving the Korean/APAC market

## When it doesn't

- Teams needing a broad global multi-region footprint for latency-sensitive, geographically distributed applications
- Users wanting pure, minimal-commitment commodity GPU rental without bundled education/platform products

## Inclusion criteria

Elice meets all three inclusion criteria: transparent pricing (dedicated Pricing page at elice.io), self-service signup (Get Started flow at elice.io/get-started and Sign In at accounts.elice.io), and a public SLA/status indication via its documented enterprise service offerings and user guide resources.
