## What makes LeaderGPU different

LeaderGPU operates as a European alternative to hyperscaler GPU clouds, built on a philosophy of owned infrastructure and no preemption guarantees. Unlike AWS, GCP, or Azure—where GPU availability is virtualized and spot instances risk termination—LeaderGPU provides bare-metal dedicated hardware in ISO 27001 & NEN 7510 certified data centers in the Netherlands. The company owns and operates all servers directly, eliminating third-party dependencies and giving customers full root access to real hardware, not VMs.

The per-minute billing model (from €0.02/min) removes commitment friction. You pay only for what you use, with no lock-in contracts, and can suspend or resume servers freely. This positions it well for researchers, startups, and teams that need GPU capacity on demand without hyperscaler pricing overhead or spot termination anxiety.

## Pricing model

LeaderGPU quotes pricing in EUR and offers flexible billing tiers:

- **Per-minute**: from €0.02/min (e.g., RTX 3090 1x @ €125/month = ~€0.087/min)
- **Daily/weekly/monthly**: fixed rates per tier
- **Minimum order**: €10, no credit card required to register
- **Entry point**: RTX 3090 (8x) from €199/month; H100 SXM (8x NVLink) at €11,900/month

Payment accepts Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, SEPA bank transfer, and Bitcoin. Pricing is significantly lower than hyperscaler on-demand GPU rates for comparable hardware (e.g., AWS p3.8xlarge with 4x V100 costs ~$24/hour vs LeaderGPU A100 SXM at ~€790/month per card). The per-minute model and transparent, all-inclusive pricing (traffic included) eliminate hidden costs.
