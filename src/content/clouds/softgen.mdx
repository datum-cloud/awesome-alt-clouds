## What makes Softgen different

Softgen is an AI application builder that distinguishes itself by prioritizing **code ownership and platform independence**, a stark contrast to many "lock-in" AI coding assistants. While competitors often trap users in proprietary environments, Softgen generates standard **Next.js** applications that are exported directly to a **GitHub repository** you own. This ensures that your intellectual property and codebase remain portable and commercially yours, with no revenue share or attribution requirements.

The platform integrates a **managed Supabase** backend, providing database, authentication, and storage out-of-the-box, while offering one-click deployment to **Vercel**. This stack allows developers to move from idea to a live, revenue-generating product in days rather than months. Unlike many AI builders that limit model selection, Softgen offers access to **12+ AI models**, including Claude, GPT, Gemini, and Kimi, allowing users to choose the best engine for their specific task.

## Pricing model

Softgen operates on a **subscription-based** model with a free trial tier.

- **Free Trial:** Includes trial credits with no credit card required.
- **Pro Plan:** Starts at **$25/month**. This tier includes:
  - Access to 12+ AI models.
  - Managed Supabase database.
  - GitHub export capabilities.
  - Credits that **never expire** while subscribed (with rollover on higher tiers).
  - Unlimited collaborators (owner pays only).

The pricing is notably competitive compared to other AI builders that charge $25–$100/month for fewer models or locked-in code. The credit system is designed for predictability, avoiding the monthly expiration penalties common in other services.
