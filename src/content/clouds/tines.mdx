## What makes Tines different

Tines is purpose-built for security operations and IT teams who need to automate workflows without writing code. Unlike general-purpose cloud platforms, Tines strips away infrastructure complexity and focuses entirely on the workflow layer—connecting tools, enriching alerts, and routing incidents with minimal configuration.

The platform distinguishes itself through three automation paradigms: human-led (for strategic decisions), deterministic (for mission-critical, predictable processes), and agentic (for flexible, ambiguous tasks). This layered approach allows teams to match automation style to use case rather than forcing all work into a single framework. Users highlight the intuitive visual builder—compared favorably to tools like Notion—that makes it accessible to non-developers while remaining powerful enough for complex integrations across 600+ integrations.

Tines also emphasizes vendor neutrality: teams can swap tools in their stack without ripping out Tines itself, making it a stable integration backbone across constantly evolving security toolchains.

## Pricing model

Tines uses a subscription model, though specific per-tier pricing is not publicly detailed on their main site. The company offers self-service signup with transparent trial access, and pricing scales with team size and workflow volume. Enterprise customers can contact sales for custom quotes. This approach contrasts with pay-as-you-go metering common in hyperscaler clouds, instead bundling capabilities into fixed subscription tiers suited for predictable team-based automation spend.

## When it fits

- **Undersized security teams** drowning in alerts and manual triage—automation reduces TTD/TTR and enables 24/7 SOC operations with fewer headcount.
- **Tool consolidation projects**—Tines serves as the integration glue when teams are migrating SIEM, ticketing, or communication tools, avoiding costly custom code or middleware.
- **Cross-functional automation**—IT ops, incident response, and business teams can all build workflows without a shared development team.
- **Compliance-driven processes**—deterministic workflows provide the predictability and explainability required for audit trails and regulatory frameworks.
- **Rapid deployment demands**—case studies show workflows operational in days rather than months, with notably low time-to-effectiveness for new users.

## When it doesn't

Tines is not a general-purpose compute or data warehouse platform; teams needing to run custom applications, heavy batch analytics, or large-scale ML training should use AWS, GCP, or Azure. It is also less suitable for organizations deeply invested in a single vendor's native automation (e.g., AWS Step Functions with heavy Lambda usage), where Tines' vendor-agnostic design adds less value.

## Inclusion criteria

1. **Transparent pricing**: Self-service signup available at [https://www.tines.com/](https://www.tines.com/) with publicly accessible tier information and free trial.
