## What makes JumpCloud different

JumpCloud positions itself as a "Cloud Directory" platform that unifies identity, access, and device management into a single pane of glass. Unlike traditional on-premises Active Directory solutions or siloed cloud-native IAM tools, JumpCloud is built entirely in the cloud, allowing it to manage heterogeneous environments seamlessly. It supports human, non-human (service accounts), and agentic identities (AI agents), providing a unified lifecycle management approach that scales from small startups to large enterprises.

The platform’s core differentiator is its ability to manage devices across all major operating systems—Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and iOS—using a single agent. This eliminates the need for separate MDM solutions for different OS families. By integrating identity proofing, multi-factor authentication (MFA), and single sign-on (SSO) with device compliance checks, JumpCloud enforces zero-trust security policies directly tied to the device state and user identity.

Furthermore, JumpCloud has recently expanded into "Agentic IAM," addressing the emerging need to govern AI agents and non-human identities. This allows organizations to grant least-privilege access to infrastructure and enterprise data for AI-driven workflows, ensuring that autonomous agents are securely onboarded, monitored, and decommissioned just like human users.

## Pricing model

JumpCloud operates on a transparent, per-user, per-month subscription model. Pricing is tiered based on the features required, with clear distinctions between core identity management and advanced device management capabilities.

*   **Free Plan:** Includes up to 10 users with basic directory and MFA capabilities.
*   **Standard:** Starts at approximately $3.25–$3.50 per user/month, adding SSO, device management, and password manager features.
*   **Premium:** Starts at approximately $6.50–$7.00 per user/month, adding advanced security features, API access, and priority support.
*   **Enterprise:** Custom pricing for large organizations requiring advanced compliance, dedicated support, and custom SLAs.

The model stands out for its simplicity and predictability compared to usage-based cloud providers. There are no hidden fees for API calls or data egress within the identity layer, making it easy to forecast IT infrastructure costs.

## When it fits

*   **Heterogeneous IT Environments:** Organizations managing a mix of Windows, macOS, Linux, and mobile devices that need a unified management tool.
*   **Remote and Distributed Workforces:** Teams requiring secure, frictionless access to cloud apps and on-premises resources from any location.
*   **Startups and SMEs:** Small to medium businesses looking for an all-in-one IT management solution that reduces the complexity of managing multiple vendor tools.
*   **Agentic AI Integration:** Enterprises exploring AI agents that need secure, governed access to enterprise data and infrastructure.

## When it doesn't

*   **Heavy Compute/Storage Workloads:** JumpCloud is an identity and device management platform, not a compute or storage provider; it does not host applications or data.
*   **On-Premises Only Environments:** While it integrates with on-premises resources, its core value is delivered via the cloud, making it less suitable for strictly air-gapped, offline-only scenarios.
