## What makes IPinfo different

IPinfo specializes exclusively in IP intelligence, whereas hyperscalers treat IP data as a secondary service bolted onto broader infrastructure offerings. The core differentiator is IPinfo's **Probe Network**—over 1,000 points of presence globally that capture real-time internet infrastructure signals rather than relying on historical or derived data. This active measurement approach enables higher accuracy in geolocation, ASN ownership, and privacy detection.

IPinfo's data model is built for developers: the API returns structured, clean JSON with optional flat-file formats (CSV, MMDB, Parquet) and direct integrations into data warehouses like Snowflake. The company publicly commits to 99.99% uptime and 50–200ms response latency, with no vendor lock-in—you can download databases and host them locally if needed. This is fundamentally different from AWS GeoIP or Azure IP Intelligence, which are tightly coupled to their cloud ecosystems.

## Pricing model

IPinfo publishes **five subscription tiers** on its pricing page:

- **IPinfo Lite**: Free tier with limited lookups (designed for evaluation)
- **IPinfo Core**: Monthly subscription; ASN, geolocation, and privacy detection included
- **IPinfo Plus**: Adds residential proxy detection and mobile carrier data
- **IPinfo Max**: Enterprise-grade with higher request volumes
- **IPinfo Enterprise**: Custom solutions and dedicated support

Exact per-request pricing and request limits are not disclosed on the public site; customers must sign up or contact sales for volumetric details. This is typical for B2B IP data vendors and contrasts with hyperscaler pay-as-you-go models. Billing is monthly subscription-based, not hourly or consumption metered.

## When it fits

- **Fraud detection & risk scoring**: Real-time VPN/proxy/Tor detection to identify high-risk login attempts or transactions
- **Compliance & abuse prevention**: Accurate geolocation and abuse contact data for content licensing, GDPR regional rules, or spam mitigation
- **Ad tech & analytics**: Understanding user location and mobile carrier for campaign targeting and attribution
- **Network operations**: ASN and Whois data for peering decisions, network troubleshooting, or ISP relationship management
- **Offline-first applications**: Download databases (JSON, MMDB) for self-hosted lookups without external API dependency

## When it doesn't

IPinfo is **not a general-purpose cloud provider**—it offers no compute, storage, or infrastructure services. It is also not suitable for workloads requiring extensive historical IP reputation data or large-scale DDoS/botnet threat feeds (those are better served by security-focused vendors like Akamai or Cloudflare).
