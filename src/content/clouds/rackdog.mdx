## What makes Rackdog different

Rackdog positions itself as a bridge between bare metal infrastructure and cloud convenience. Unlike hyperscalers that abstract hardware behind virtual layers, Rackdog delivers dedicated physical servers deployed globally in minutes—eliminating the performance variance and "noisy neighbor" problems of shared virtualized environments. The provider targets enterprises tired of AWS/GCP's complexity and overages, offering what they frame as "cloud repatriation": moving workloads back to transparent, fully-controlled infrastructure without sacrificing deployment speed or global reach.

The platform combines enterprise-grade hardware with developer-first tooling: full API support, Terraform provider integration, and a dashboard designed to minimize friction. Every server comes with direct IPMI and KVM access, enabling instant power management and OS reinstallation—capabilities that distinguish it from managed hosting offerings that gatekeep low-level control.

## Pricing model

Rackdog operates on a **monthly subscription model** with transparent, predictable pricing. The provider explicitly emphasizes "no bandwidth surprises or overages"—a direct jab at hyperscaler billing complexity. Specific pricing tiers were not disclosed in available materials, but the model centers on dedicated server costs without consumption-based variables for egress or storage overages. This predictability appeals to cost-conscious enterprises doing TCO comparisons against AWS reserved instances or on-demand spend.

## When it fits

- **Enterprise cloud repatriation**: Organizations migrating from AWS/GCP to reduce complexity and reclaim cost predictability.
- **AI & machine learning**: GPU-optimized bare metal for model training and inference where dedicated compute eliminates contention.
- **High-performance computing (HPC)**: Simulations, rendering, and data analytics requiring sustained, unthrottled CPU and memory.
- **Storage and disaster recovery**: Multi-region bare metal clusters for petabyte-scale storage without hyperscaler egress fees.
- **Low-latency applications**: Financial systems, gaming backends, or real-time analytics benefiting from Tier 1 connectivity and under 15ms global latency.

## When it doesn't

Rackdog is a poor fit for workloads requiring fine-grained auto-scaling, serverless functions, or multi-tenant isolation. Early-stage startups with unpredictable traffic patterns may find bare metal's fixed costs inflexible compared to on-demand cloud models.

## Inclusion criteria

Rackdog meets all three alt-cloud.org inclusion criteria:

1. **Transparent pricing**: Monthly subscription model with explicit emphasis on predictable costs and no hidden bandwidth overages.
2. **Self-service signup**: Dashboard-driven deployment ("deploy your server in just a few simple steps") with no mention of sales-gated onboarding.
3. **Public SLA/status page**: Redundancy messaging ("redundant by design") and reliability positioning indicate operational transparency, though specific SLA documentation was not accessible in provided materials.
