## What makes A2Agent different

A2Agent positions itself as an aggregation layer rather than a foundation model provider — it routes requests across multiple Chinese and open LLM providers (DeepSeek, GLM, Kimi, Qwen, MiniMax) through a single OpenAI-, Anthropic- or Gemini-compatible API. This lets developers switch models without rewriting integration code, which is useful for teams hedging against single-vendor pricing or availability risk.

The platform emphasizes privacy and risk controls: it states that request and response bodies for ordinary API traffic are not stored, and that prompts and model outputs are never used to train models (the upstream vendor's own retention policy still applies). Combined with failover across its own vendor accounts and a public status page tracking provider health, the pitch is reliable, lower-cost access to Asian LLMs for a global developer audience.

Rather than owning infrastructure, A2Agent's value is in unifying billing, API compatibility, and access — a pattern common to LLM gateway/aggregator products that compete on price and convenience rather than raw compute.

## Pricing model

A2Agent uses a usage-based, pay-as-you-go model with no monthly fee or minimum spend; top-ups start at $5, and credits do not expire while the account is active. Two tiers are offered:

- **Developer**: pay-as-you-go access to GLM, Kimi, DeepSeek (including deep-thinking mode), Qwen, and MiniMax — aimed at individuals and students.
- **Enterprise**: volume-based top-up discounts, dedicated concurrency limits, higher requests-per-minute caps, and access to every model/provider channel — aimed at SaaS companies and teams. Enterprise requires contacting sales.

The pricing page lists per-model discounts of roughly 10-50% below official list prices, with the final rate depending on the model and API key group, plus bonus credit on larger top-ups. Payments are processed via Stripe (including bank cards), and every new account starts with $2 in free credit, no card required. This differs from typical hyperscaler pricing by removing fixed infrastructure costs entirely — you pay only per token/request, with no compute reservations or tiers to manage.

## When it fits

- Developers who want a single API key/endpoint to access multiple Chinese/open-weight LLMs (DeepSeek, GLM, Kimi, Qwen, MiniMax).
- Teams building on OpenAI- or Anthropic-compatible tooling (e.g., Claude Code, Codex) who want to swap in cheaper models with minimal code changes.
- Startups or individual developers wanting free trial credits and no monthly commitment.
- Projects sensitive to per-model pricing that want to shop across providers via one billing relationship.

## When it doesn't

- Teams needing guaranteed data residency, compliance certifications, or dedicated infrastructure beyond what a routing gateway can offer.
- Workloads requiring proprietary Western foundation models (e.g., GPT-4-class OpenAI/Anthropic models) not listed among the supported model set.

## Inclusion criteria

A2Agent meets all three criteria: **transparent pricing** (stated discount range and tier structure on the pricing page), **self-service signup** (free account creation and API key generation at https://a2agent.me/register), and a **public status page** (https://a2agent.me/status, showing per-provider health and 7-day uptime).
