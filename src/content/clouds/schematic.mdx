## What makes Schematic different

Schematic addresses a specific pain point in the SaaS development lifecycle: the complexity of managing monetization logic. Unlike hyperscalers that provide generic compute or storage, Schematic offers a specialized infrastructure layer for billing. It decouples pricing models from application code, allowing growth and product teams to iterate on pricing, packaging, and entitlements without requiring engineering releases.

The platform is built on top of Stripe, leveraging its robust payment infrastructure while adding a layer of logic for usage metering, feature gating, and plan management. By providing SDKs for React, Go, Node.js, Python, and Java, Schematic enables developers to check user entitlements and limits in real-time with low latency (under 50ms). This approach shifts the burden of billing maintenance away from core product engineering, treating pricing as a configurable product feature rather than a hard-coded backend requirement.

## When it fits

*   **SaaS Companies with Complex Pricing:** Teams offering usage-based, seat-based, or hybrid pricing models that require frequent iteration.
*   **Product-Led Growth (PLG):** Organizations needing self-serve trials, paywalls, and upgrade paths without engineering intervention.
*   **Monetization Expansion:** Businesses looking to add new features as paid add-ons or manage entitlements across multiple plans.
*   **Stripe Users:** Companies already using Stripe that want to reduce webhook management and integration complexity.

## When it doesn't

*   **General Cloud Infrastructure:** This provider does not offer compute, storage, or networking services; it is strictly a billing/entitlement layer.
*   **Simple Flat-Rate Models:** Businesses with static, one-time pricing may find the overhead of a dedicated entitlement platform unnecessary.
