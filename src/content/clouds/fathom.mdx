## What makes Fathom different

Fathom is built as an intentional alternative to Google Analytics, prioritizing simplicity and privacy over feature bloat and data extraction. Unlike hyperscalers that monetize user data through advertising and tracking networks, Fathom operates on a transparent subscription model where customers are the only stakeholders. The platform anonymizes IP addresses and operates entirely cookie-free, making it compliant with GDPR, CCPA, ePrivacy, and PECR without requiring visitors to consent to tracking or accept cookie banners.

The product is designed for developers and non-technical founders alike. Setup requires a single line of code that works with any website, CMS, or framework—no training certifications or complex configurations. The lightweight 2kB script integrates with WordPress, Next.js, Vue.js, Webflow, ConvertKit, Discourse, and dozens of other platforms. Data retention is permanent; users retain access to historical analytics indefinitely, unlike Google Analytics' sampling and deletion policies.

## Pricing model

Fathom pricing starts at **$15/month** and scales with pageview volume. A free 7-day trial is available with no credit card required. The company operates independently without venture funding or external investors, meaning pricing decisions are driven solely by customer value rather than growth-at-all-costs pressures. This contrasts sharply with freemium analytics tools that subsidize free tiers through data monetization.

## When it fits

- **Privacy-conscious organizations**: Sites handling sensitive visitor data, GDPR-regulated businesses in the EU, or those wanting to respect user privacy without legal obligation.
- **Small-to-medium websites**: Blogs, SaaS products, and marketing sites that need actionable insights without Google Analytics' overwhelming report density.
- **Content creators and indie developers**: Solo founders and small teams valuing simplicity over advanced segmentation; the straightforward dashboard requires no learning curve.
- **Performance-sensitive applications**: Projects where lightweight script weight (2kB) and zero third-party tracking improve Core Web Vitals and SEO rankings.
- **Long-term data retention requirements**: Organizations needing permanent historical data for trend analysis across years or decades.

## When it doesn't

Fathom lacks advanced audience segmentation, funnel analysis, and machine learning-driven anomaly detection that enterprise analytics platforms provide. Organizations requiring complex cohort analysis, multivariate testing infrastructure, or deep integration with data warehouses may find it limiting.
