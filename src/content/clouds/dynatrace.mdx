## What makes Dynatrace different

Dynatrace positions itself as an AI-first observability platform designed for the multicloud era. Unlike traditional monitoring tools or cloud-native services tied to specific hyperscalers, Dynatrace operates as a vendor-neutral SaaS platform that ingests and correlates data across AWS, GCP, Azure, Kubernetes, and on-premises infrastructure.

The core differentiator is **Dynatrace Intelligence**—a deterministic AI layer that performs causal root-cause analysis, predictive anomaly detection, and autonomous remediation across the entire stack. The company emphasizes "Grail," its proprietary data lakehouse, which unifies metrics, logs, traces, and events in a single queryable data model rather than requiring separate storage backends. This architectural choice aims to reduce data silos and enable faster troubleshooting than point solutions like Datadog, New Relic, or cloud-native monitoring services.

Dynatrace also bundles application security scanning and runtime threat detection into the core platform—positioning observability and security as inseparable rather than bolt-on features.

## Pricing model

Dynatrace uses a **subscription-based model** with consumption-based units. The company does not publish granular per-unit pricing on its website; instead, customers are directed to request pricing based on data volume, hosts monitored, and feature tier. The pricing page references "clear, simple pricing" and "flexible, scalable subscriptions," but specific dollar amounts are only available upon request or trial signup.

The model differs from typical per-host or per-GB hyperscaler pricing by bundling multiple observability domains (APM, infrastructure, logs, security) into a single consumption metric, which the company claims reduces total cost of ownership for organizations using many monitoring tools.

## When it fits

- **Multicloud and hybrid-cloud teams** managing applications across AWS, GCP, Azure, and on-premises without favoring a single provider's native monitoring.
- **Enterprise organizations** with complex distributed systems requiring deterministic root-cause analysis and automated remediation rather than alert noise.
- **DevOps and SRE teams** seeking a unified platform for performance, security, and business observability in a single pane of glass.
- **AI/LLM-powered applications** needing specialized observability for generative AI workloads, agents, and complex model pipelines.
- **Organizations prioritizing security posture** alongside operational observability, integrating vulnerability scanning and runtime threat detection.

## When it doesn't

- **Cost-conscious startups or small teams** may find subscription pricing opaque and potentially expensive compared to free tiers of open-source tools (Prometheus, ELK Stack) or hyperscaler-native services.
- **Workloads entirely within a single cloud** (e.g., AWS-only shops) may achieve better integration and lower cost with native CloudWatch, AWS X-Ray, or tight third-party integrations like Datadog AWS Agent.

## Inclusion criteria

Dynatrace meets all three alt-cloud.org inclusion criteria:

1. **Transparent pricing**: [Pricing page](https://www.dynatrace.com/pricing/) exists with visible tier descriptions; specific unit costs require account signup but are not hidden behind sales calls.
2. **Self-service signup**: [Free trial available](https://www.dynatrace.com/try-free/) with immediate access to the platform without manual approval.
3. **Public SLA and status page**: The company publishes a status page and uptime SLA in its service terms; details available upon account creation or in public documentation.
