## What makes Sumo Logic different

Sumo Logic is purpose-built for cloud-native observability and security operations, not a retrofitted on-premises tool. The platform ingests machine data (logs, metrics, traces) at scale and applies proprietary ML algorithms and generative AI (Dojo AI) to detect threats and resolve operational issues automatically. Unlike point solutions, Sumo Logic consolidates security and operations monitoring into a single data lake, reducing tool sprawl and investigation time for teams running containerized and cloud workloads.

The platform natively supports OpenTelemetry and ships pre-built integration apps for AWS, GCP, Azure, Kubernetes, and dozens of popular SaaS tools. This reduces onboarding friction compared to platforms that require heavy custom configuration. Sumo Logic also competes directly against Splunk, Datadog, Elastic, and Google SecOps—positioning itself as a cost-competitive alternative with faster time-to-value for mid-market and enterprise customers.

## Pricing model

Sumo Logic uses a usage-based pricing model tied to data ingestion volume. Specific per-GB rates are not publicly listed; instead, customers provision daily ingestion capacity (typically quoted per 100 GB, 500 GB, or custom limits) on an annual or monthly subscription. A 30-day free trial is available to test the platform with pre-built dashboards and apps.

This approach differs from Splunk's licensing (per-GB indexed), favoring teams with predictable, moderate data volumes. For high-volume log generators (e.g., large Kubernetes clusters), costs can escalate; Sumo Logic's strength is teams seeking all-in-one observability without the hyperscaler markup.

## When it fits

- **Cloud-native security teams** running SIEM, threat detection, or compliance workloads (PCI, FedRAMP) who need faster threat response than traditional tools
- **DevOps and SRE teams** monitoring Kubernetes, microservices, and multi-cloud infrastructure with unified dashboards and automation
- **Mid-market SaaS and fintech companies** seeking tool consolidation and lower total cost of ownership than Splunk or Datadog
- **Organizations needing AI-powered investigation** (Dojo AI) to automate log analysis and incident triage at scale
- **Regulated industries** (finance, public sector, healthcare) requiring audit trails and compliance integrations built into the platform

## When it doesn't

- **Single-region, on-premises-first organizations** will find limited value; Sumo Logic assumes cloud-first operations.
- **Teams with extremely high log volumes** (multiple petabytes/day) may find usage-based pricing uncompetitive vs. Splunk or in-house ELK stacks.
