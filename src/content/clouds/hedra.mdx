## What makes Hedra different

Hedra positions itself less as a general-purpose cloud and more as a research lab with infrastructure attached. Rather than renting raw compute, Hedra pairs GPU capacity with "Forward Deployed Researchers" who work directly with customer teams through its Model Factory program to post-train, evaluate, and serve multimodal models that the customer ultimately owns. This is a meaningfully different model from hyperscalers, which sell undifferentiated compute and expect customers to bring their own ML expertise.

The company's technical focus is on the hard problems of inference for world and action models — optimizing operations like attention across video frames and studying how lower numerical precision trades off against GPU time and output quality. This work is informed by Hedra's own production video generation models, which it has deployed in its consumer-facing app, Play, giving the research team direct feedback from a live, high-throughput product.

Hedra also offers sovereign deployment options and dedicated GPU Sandboxes, targeting teams (including physical AI and defense use cases) that need to train or serve models in controlled or air-gapped environments rather than multi-tenant public cloud infrastructure.

## Pricing model

Hedra publishes pricing on a dedicated page (hedra.com/pricing) covering its Inference, Model training, and GPU Sandbox products, though specific per-hour or per-token rates were not captured from the pages retrieved here. The model is usage-based, spanning self-serve inference/API consumption and higher-touch, negotiated engagements for Model Factory and sovereign deployments. What stands out versus typical hyperscaler pricing is the bundling of research labor (forward-deployed researchers) alongside compute, meaning cost reflects not just GPU-hours but applied ML expertise aimed at a shippable, owned model.

## When it fits

- Teams building or fine-tuning multimodal (video/audio/action) models who want hands-on research support, not just raw GPU rental.
- Organizations that need to retain ownership of post-trained models rather than depending on a third party's hosted model.
- Physical AI, robotics, and defense teams needing sovereign or isolated deployment of custom models.
- Media and creative companies wanting production-grade video/world-model inference optimization.
- Teams that want GPU sandboxes for rapid experimentation before committing to larger training runs.

## When it doesn't

- Not a fit for generic, commodity compute needs (simple VM hosting, storage, or non-ML workloads) where a standard IaaS provider is cheaper and simpler.
- Not ideal for teams wanting fully self-serve, hands-off infrastructure with no research collaboration — Hedra's value is tied closely to its applied research engagement model.

## Inclusion criteria

Hedra meets all three inclusion criteria: transparent pricing (published at [hedra.com/pricing](https://www.hedra.com/pricing)), self-service signup (via [hedra.com/auth/start?flow=signup](https://www.hedra.com/auth/start?flow=signup)), and a public status page ([status.hedra.com](https://status.hedra.com/)).
