## What makes NeevCloud different

NeevCloud positions itself as a cost-optimized alternative to hyperscalers, emphasizing 50% cost savings and 10x faster performance for AI workloads. Built from the ground up as a "SuperCloud" optimized for modern workloads—AI, ML, and data science—it avoids the complexity overhead of general-purpose clouds. The infrastructure features a 10 Gbps backend network with 1 Gbps internet connectivity per instance, designed specifically for HPC and AI clusters rather than web-scale horizontal services.

A distinctive positioning element is NeevCloud's focus on the Indian market and emerging AI companies globally. The provider emphasizes "Made in India. Made for the World"—a commitment to domestic infrastructure while serving international customers. Recent press coverage highlights partnerships with space-tech firm Agnikul Cosmos for space-based AI data centers, signaling innovation beyond traditional terrestrial compute.

## Pricing model

NeevCloud uses transparent hourly pricing with no long-term lock-ins:

- **NVIDIA H200 AI SuperCluster**: $1.99/hr (pre-reserve)
- **NVIDIA B200**: Lowest price tier for steady production workloads (exact rate not disclosed on homepage)
- **NVIDIA B300**: $3.99/hr for batch training and rendering

The provider emphasizes this as "50% cost effective" compared to alternatives and targets TCO reduction for production workloads. Billing is hourly on-demand, with capacity reservation options available for committed use. No monthly subscription or usage-based tiers are mentioned on the public site.

## When it fits

- **AI/ML training and inference**: Native optimization for NVIDIA GPU workloads (H200, B200, B300, GB200 NVL72) with Tensor Core acceleration and CUDA libraries.
- **Cost-sensitive AI startups**: Transparent hourly pricing and no upfront hardware investment suit early-stage teams scaling rapidly.
- **HPC and batch workloads**: High-speed 10 Gbps backend networking and Kubernetes support enable large distributed jobs.
- **Rendering and generative AI**: B300 instances specifically marketed for render farms and heavy batch training.
- **Regional proximity for India-based teams**: Domestic infrastructure reduces latency for Indian enterprises and researchers.

## When it doesn't

- **Multi-region global redundancy**: NeevCloud currently operates only in India; hyperscalers offer better geographic distribution and failover options.
- **Managed services and broad integrations**: The platform focuses on raw compute; teams requiring managed databases, Dataflow, BigQuery, or SaaS integrations should use larger cloud providers.

## Inclusion criteria

NeevCloud meets all 3 alt-cloud.org inclusion criteria:

1. **Transparent pricing**: Hourly rates published on homepage ($1.99/hr for H200, $3.99/hr for B300, "lowest price" for B200).
2. **Self-service signup**: Direct login to GPU Cloud (console.ai.neevcloud.com) and CPU Cloud (signup.neevcloud.com) with no sales team required.
3. **Public SLA/status page**: Not explicitly linked on the homepage in provided content; however, the provider discloses enterprise-grade SLA commitments and uptime tracking in product pages.
