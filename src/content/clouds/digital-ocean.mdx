## Why developers pick Digital Ocean

Digital Ocean carved its niche by being the cloud you can fit in your head. While hyperscalers ship hundreds of services with intricate pricing matrices, Digital Ocean offers a focused product surface — compute, storage, databases, Kubernetes, and a PaaS — each with flat monthly pricing that you can predict on the back of a napkin.

## Pricing model

Droplets start at **$4/month** for a 512 MB shared-CPU instance and scale up to high-memory and storage-optimized configurations. Bandwidth is bundled (1–10 TB/month included per Droplet) instead of metered separately, which removes the most common surprise on a hyperscaler bill.

Managed Databases start at **$15/month** for a single-node Postgres development instance. Spaces object storage is **$5/month** for 250 GB plus 1 TB outbound transfer, then $0.02/GB.

## When it makes sense

- **Startups and indie hackers** who need a predictable monthly run-rate
- **Side projects and prototypes** that fit on one or two Droplets
- **Teams adopting Kubernetes** without wanting to learn an opinionated managed Kubernetes flavor
- **Geographies where hyperscalers are expensive** — Asia-Pacific and Europe pricing remains competitive

## When it might not

If your workload demands deep PaaS abstractions (Lambda + DynamoDB style), or sovereign-cloud certifications, the hyperscalers still have an edge. Digital Ocean's strength is the boring middle of the stack done well.

## Inclusion criteria

Digital Ocean meets all three criteria:

- **Transparent public pricing** — full price list on the marketing site, no sales call required
- **Self-service signup** — credit card, sign up, deploy in under five minutes
- **Public SLA** — [99.99% uptime SLA](https://www.digitalocean.com/legal/sla) with service credits and a public [status page](https://status.digitalocean.com/)
