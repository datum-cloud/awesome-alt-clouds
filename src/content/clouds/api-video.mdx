## What makes api.video different

api.video is a specialized infrastructure provider focused exclusively on video workflows, distinguishing itself from general-purpose hyperscalers by offering a unified API for the entire video lifecycle. While AWS MediaConvert or Azure Media Services require stitching together multiple disparate services for encoding, storage, delivery, and analytics, api.video provides a single interface to upload, encode, host, stream, and analyze video content. This "all-in-one" approach significantly reduces integration complexity for developers building video-centric applications, such as learning management systems or social media platforms.

The platform emphasizes speed and ease of integration, boasting over 140 points of presence (PoPs) for global delivery and a claimed playback speed of 0.02s. It supports a wide variety of client SDKs and plugins, allowing developers to integrate video capabilities into existing tech stacks with minimal code. Furthermore, api.video has integrated AI features directly into the API, enabling instant transcription and summarization of videos via simple API calls, a feature that typically requires separate AI service integrations on other clouds.

## Pricing model

api.video operates on a usage-based pricing model with a generous free tier, making it accessible for startups and individual developers. The free plan allows for the storage of up to 500 videos, 500GB of bandwidth, and 20 hours of live streaming monthly.

Paid plans scale with usage, typically categorized by bandwidth consumption and video storage limits. While specific per-GB pricing varies by plan tier, the model is designed to be transparent and predictable, avoiding the complex egress fees often associated with hyperscaler object storage and CDN services. Volume discounts are available for high-traffic applications. This model stands out by bundling encoding, storage, and delivery into a single metric (bandwidth/storage), simplifying cost forecasting for video-heavy apps.

## When it fits

- **Video-First Applications:** Startups or enterprises building platforms like online learning portals, video marketplaces, or social media apps that require rapid integration of video playback and live streaming.
- **Developer Efficiency:** Teams that want to avoid the operational overhead of managing multiple cloud services for video encoding, CDN configuration, and analytics.
- **AI-Enhanced Video:** Use cases requiring automatic video transcription and summarization for accessibility or content discovery without managing separate AI infrastructure.
- **Global Delivery Needs:** Applications requiring low-latency video delivery to a global audience, leveraging api.video's extensive CDN network.

## When it doesn't

- **General-Purpose Cloud Workloads:** This provider is not suitable for general computing, database, or networking needs.
- **Custom Video Pipeline Control:** Enterprises requiring deep, low-level control over every step of the video encoding pipeline or custom codec implementations may find the managed API too restrictive compared to self-hosted solutions on generic cloud infrastructure.
