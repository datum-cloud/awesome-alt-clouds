## What makes Samsara different

Samsara is not a traditional cloud infrastructure provider like AWS or Azure; it is an end-to-end Internet of Things (IoT) platform designed for physical operations. While hyperscalers provide the raw compute and storage layers, Samsara provides the specialized hardware, connectivity, and application layer specifically built to digitize trucks, warehouses, and construction sites. Its core differentiator is the integration of proprietary hardware (GPS trackers, industrial sensors, dashcams) with a unified software platform that processes over 80 billion miles of data annually.

The platform leverages edge computing on devices to perform real-time AI inference for safety detections (such as driver distraction or hazardous weather) before sending actionable insights to the cloud. This "hardware-in-the-loop" approach allows for immediate operational interventions that generic cloud providers cannot offer out of the box. Samsara focuses on vertical-specific workflows for fleet management, safety compliance, and asset utilization, rather than general-purpose cloud services.

## Pricing model

Samsara operates on a subscription-based model that typically bundles hardware costs with monthly software licensing fees. Specific per-unit pricing is not publicly listed on the homepage and requires contacting sales for a quote, though the model is generally structured around the number of connected devices (vehicles or assets) and the feature tier selected.

The pricing stands out for its transparency in value rather than granular compute billing. Customers pay for the integrated ecosystem—hardware, cellular connectivity, and software analytics—rather than paying separately for data ingestion, storage, and API calls as is common in IaaS/PaaS models. This simplifies budgeting for operations teams who prefer predictable operational expenditures (OpEx) over variable cloud usage costs.

## When it fits

- **Fleet Management:** Companies needing real-time GPS tracking, route optimization, and driver safety monitoring for trucks or delivery vehicles.
- **Industrial Safety:** Construction sites, warehouses, or manufacturing plants requiring worker safety monitoring, geofencing, and hazard detection.
- **Asset Visibility:** Organizations needing to track high-value or frequently moved assets (e.g., containers, generators) across multiple locations.
- **Predictive Maintenance:** Teams looking to reduce downtime by monitoring equipment health (temperature, vibration, engine diagnostics) in real-time.

## When it doesn't

- **General Cloud Infrastructure:** Samsara does not offer virtual machines, object storage, or serverless computing functions, making it unsuitable for hosting web applications or processing generic data workloads.
- **Pure Software IoT:** If you already own IoT hardware and only need a backend for data ingestion and visualization, Samsara’s bundled hardware requirement may be unnecessary and cost-prohibitive compared to open-source or generic IoT platforms.
