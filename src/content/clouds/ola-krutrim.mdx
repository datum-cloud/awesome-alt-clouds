## What makes Ola Krutrim different

Ola Krutrim is positioning itself as India's first sovereign AI cloud, addressing growing regulatory and geopolitical concerns around data residency and cloud infrastructure control. Unlike AWS, GCP, or Azure, Krutrim is designed with a domestic-first architecture where all data stays within Indian borders—a critical advantage for organizations subject to Indian data sovereignty mandates or seeking to avoid foreign cloud dependencies.

The platform is built specifically for AI workloads at scale, with purpose-built infrastructure including NVIDIA A100 and H100 GPUs, high-throughput InfiniBand networking for GPU cluster interconnects, and NVMe-optimized storage designed for ML data pipelines. Krutrim distinguishes itself by claiming production-grade reliability proven at scale—the platform already powers consumer, industrial, and public systems with thousands of VMs and petabytes of migrated data, giving it real-world operational credibility beyond typical beta offerings.

Developer experience is a core focus, with AWS-compatible APIs enabling zero-friction migration, SDKs in Python, Node.js, Go, Java, and Rust, and transparent INR-denominated billing. The platform also targets emerging AI use cases like MCP servers and agentic workloads, positioning itself ahead of traditional hyperscaler feature parity.

## Pricing model

Krutrim uses a transparent, usage-based pricing model with INR billing and itemized invoices. While specific per-hour rates are not published on their public website, the platform advertises under 30% lower infrastructure costs compared to typical cloud alternatives, with A100 instances specifically noted as "competitively priced." Pricing is transparent and real-time cost tracking is available through the console, with usage alerts to prevent bill surprises. The model favors on-demand and reserved instance options for GPU compute, with separate pricing for storage and networking.

## When it fits

- **AI/ML workloads requiring data sovereignty**: Training and inference for LLMs, diffusion models, and computer vision systems where Indian data residency is mandatory or preferred.
- **Large-scale production AI infrastructure**: Organizations migrating from hyperscalers or building new systems that need proven, production-tested GPU clusters with high availability.
- **Cost-sensitive AI operations**: Teams seeking 20–30% infrastructure savings on GPU-heavy workloads compared to AWS or GCP.
- **Fast experimentation for developers**: Solo researchers and startup teams building AI models with simplified onboarding and competitive per-GPU pricing.
- **Regulated industries in India**: Financial services, healthcare, and government agencies requiring domestic cloud infrastructure with SOC 2, ISO 27001, and ISO 27018 compliance.

## When it doesn't

Krutrim is not yet a full-stack replacement for AWS or Azure. Organizations requiring extensive managed services, multi-region global failover beyond India, or deep integrations with non-AI workloads (databases, CDNs, application servers) will find more mature offerings elsewhere. Early-stage platform means ecosystem and third-party integrations are still developing.

## Inclusion criteria

Ola Krutrim meets all three alt-cloud.org inclusion criteria:

1. **Transparent pricing**: Usage-based model with INR denominated billing and itemized invoices visible in the console.
2. **Self-service signup**: Developer-first onboarding with intuitive cloud console and one-click deployment.
3. **Public SLA & status page**: Financially backed uptime SLA with round-the-clock support and advanced observability integrated into the platform.
