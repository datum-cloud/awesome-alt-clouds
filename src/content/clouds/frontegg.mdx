## What makes Frontegg different

Frontegg distinguishes itself by focusing exclusively on the identity layer for SaaS applications, rather than offering a broad Infrastructure-as-a-Service (IaaS) or Platform-as-a-Service (PaaS) suite. While hyperscalers provide the underlying compute and storage, Frontegg handles the complex, time-consuming task of building and maintaining secure user management systems. It operates as a dedicated Identity-as-a-Service (IDaaS) provider that integrates directly into your application via SDKs and APIs, allowing developers to ship features like sign-up, login, and profile management in days rather than months.

The platform is designed for speed and compliance out of the box. It includes pre-built components for authentication, authorization, and user self-service portals, significantly reducing the development overhead associated with building custom identity solutions. Frontegg also emphasizes security and privacy compliance (such as GDPR and CCPA) natively, which is critical for customer-facing applications handling sensitive user data.

Recently, Frontegg has expanded into AI with "Agen.co," an enterprise-grade agentic layer that allows users to interact with SaaS products through generative AI platforms safely. This positions Frontegg not just as an identity provider, but as a gateway for secure AI integration, ensuring that AI interactions with your application are authenticated, authorized, and auditable.

## Pricing model

Frontegg operates on a subscription-based pricing model, primarily driven by the number of Monthly Active Users (MAU). They offer a "Starter" plan that is free for up to 500 MAUs, which is ideal for early-stage startups or internal tools. Paid tiers scale with user volume and include additional features such as advanced security, custom branding, and dedicated support.

Unlike typical cloud providers that charge for compute hours or data egress, Frontegg's costs are predictable and tied directly to your user base. This makes budgeting simpler for SaaS businesses, as costs correlate directly with revenue-generating users. The free tier serves as a strong barrier to entry for competitors who require immediate payment setups, allowing developers to prototype and validate their identity needs without financial commitment.

## When it fits

*   **SaaS Startups:** Teams that need to ship a secure, compliant user management system quickly without hiring specialized security engineers.
*   **B2B Applications:** Companies requiring robust Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) and tenant isolation for multi-tenant architectures.
*   **Compliance-Heavy Industries:** Businesses that need to adhere to strict data privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA) with pre-built compliance features.
*   **AI-Integrated Products:** Developers looking to safely integrate generative AI agents into their applications with proper identity verification.

## When it doesn't

Frontegg is not a substitute for general cloud infrastructure; it does not provide compute, storage, or database services. If you are looking for a full-stack cloud provider to host your entire application, you will still need to pair Frontegg with an IaaS/PaaS provider like AWS, GCP, or Azure.
