## What makes Firefly different

Firefly positions itself not as an infrastructure provider, but as an intelligent orchestration layer for existing cloud environments. Unlike hyperscalers that provide raw compute and storage, Firefly focuses on "Cloud Resilience" by automating the management, governance, and recovery of infrastructure-as-code (IaC). It acts as a "System of Record" that ingests your current cloud state, converts ungoverned resources into IaC, and maintains strict compliance with standards like SOC 2, ISO, and DORA.

The core differentiator is its "Agentic Workflows" powered by Thinkerbell AI. These agents operate with full context of your infrastructure, enabling automated remediation of drifts and misconfigurations without manual runbooks. In the event of a cyberattack or outage, Firefly’s agents can instantly rebuild environments into clean, isolated states, significantly reducing Mean Time to Recovery (MTTR) and ensuring business continuity. This shifts the paradigm from reactive backup-and-restore to proactive, automated resilience.

## Pricing model

Specific per-unit pricing is not publicly disclosed on the main landing page, as Firefly typically targets enterprise clients with custom subscription models. However, the service operates on a subscription basis rather than a usage-based or hourly model. This contrasts with typical cloud infrastructure pricing, which scales directly with consumption. For enterprise teams, this predictable cost structure can simplify budgeting for resilience and governance tools compared to variable IaC tooling or emergency incident response costs.

## When it fits

- **Enterprise CloudOps:** Teams managing complex, multi-cloud or large-scale AWS/Azure environments that struggle with IaC sprawl and drift.
- **Security & Compliance-First Orgs:** Organizations requiring continuous compliance evidence for SOC 2, ISO 27001, or DORA regulations.
- **High-Risk Infrastructure:** Companies needing automated disaster recovery and rapid re-provisioning capabilities to mitigate ransomware or catastrophic outages.
- **DevOps Efficiency:** SRE teams looking to eliminate manual runbooks and automate standard infrastructure provisioning with guardrails.

## When it doesn't

- **Small Startups:** Early-stage companies with simple, single-account infrastructure may find the enterprise-grade governance and AI agent overhead unnecessary.
- **Raw Compute Needs:** This is not a provider for hosting applications directly; it is a management layer for infrastructure hosted elsewhere.
