## What makes Akamai different

Akamai operates one of the world's most distributed edge networks, spanning thousands of edge locations globally. Unlike hyperscalers that consolidate compute in a handful of mega-regions, Akamai brings computation, storage, and AI inference closer to end users through its edge-first architecture. This approach reduces latency for interactive applications, real-time AI workloads, and media streaming.

The platform combines compute services with integrated security and content delivery, allowing developers to build, secure, and scale applications without piecing together separate vendors. Akamai Inference Cloud specifically targets latency-sensitive AI/ML workloads by running inference at the edge rather than routing requests to centralized data centers.

Akamai's strength lies in serving performance-critical use cases—gaming, media delivery, financial services, and real-time AI—where milliseconds matter and geographic distribution is a technical requirement, not an option.

## Pricing model

Akamai employs a usage-based pricing model across its cloud and CDN services. While exact rates vary by service tier and region, the platform offers pay-as-you-go pricing for compute (CPU/GPU), storage (block and object), and data transfer. Akamai Inference Cloud pricing is consumption-based, scaling with inference requests and model size.

The company provides a [pricing list](https://www.akamai.com/us/en/products/cloud-computing/pricing/) on its website and encourages direct contact with sales for custom enterprise agreements. This approach differs from AWS/GCP's published rate cards by requiring more direct engagement, though it can yield volume discounts for large-scale deployments.

## When it fits

- **Edge-heavy applications**: Real-time gaming, live streaming, or media delivery where latency must be minimized globally.
- **Distributed AI inference**: Models requiring sub-100ms response times across geographies, leveraging Akamai Inference Cloud.
- **Security-first workloads**: Applications needing integrated DDoS protection, API security, and zero-trust architecture without multi-vendor integration.
- **Content delivery with compute**: Sites or services combining CDN, edge caching, and serverless logic (EdgeWorkers) in a single platform.
- **Compliance in regulated regions**: Organizations needing data residency or edge processing in specific geographies where Akamai has local presence.

## When it doesn't

Akamai is less suitable for workloads requiring massive, sustained compute resources in a single region or those dependent on deep integrations with AWS/GCP ecosystems. Startups seeking low-touch, fully managed infrastructure-as-code may find the platform's sales-driven onboarding friction compared to hyperscalers.

## Inclusion criteria

Akamai meets all three inclusion criteria:

1. **Transparent pricing**: [Pricing list available online](https://www.akamai.com/us/en/products/cloud-computing/pricing/) with usage-based rates published.
2. **Self-service signup**: Developers can create accounts and access the platform via the Cloud Manager interface without mandatory sales calls.
3. **Public SLA & status page**: [Service Status page](https://www.akamai.com/) available; SLAs documented for enterprise services.
