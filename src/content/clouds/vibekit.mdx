## What makes VibeKit different

VibeKit is not a hyperscaler but an AI-native app platform built around the idea that every application should have its own persistent AI agent — one that doesn't disappear after the initial build. The agent retains memory of the codebase, past bugs, and architecture decisions, and runs health checks every 60 seconds, automatically diagnosing crashes, applying fixes, and redeploying without human intervention.

The core architectural decision that sets VibeKit apart from competitors like Lovable, Bolt, Replit, and Base44 is its BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) model: instead of bundling AI usage into inflated subscription tiers, VibeKit charges only for the platform and infrastructure, while users bring their own Anthropic, OpenAI, or ChatGPT Plus (OAuth) credentials and pay model providers directly at standard rates.

It's also built for multi-interface access — web IDE, iOS app, REST API, and CLI/MCP (for tools like Cursor and Claude Code) — so apps can be built, deployed, and fixed from a phone as easily as from a terminal.

## Pricing model

VibeKit offers a free tier (10 AI sessions/month, 2 hosted apps) plus two paid subscription tiers:

- **Builder — $24.99/mo**: $20/mo AI credit included, 3 hosted apps (1 always-on), AI auto-healing + monitoring, custom domain, 24h logs, unlimited AI sessions.
- **Pro — $49.99/mo**: $25/mo AI credit + unlimited sessions, 10 apps (3 always-on, 1GB each), unlimited custom domains, 7-day logs, API access.

Users can also skip subscriptions entirely via pay-as-you-go balance top-ups starting at $10. Because of BYOK, AI token costs are separate from the platform fee — if you already pay for an AI subscription, that cost is "$0 extra" on VibeKit's side. This stands in contrast to typical cloud/SaaS pricing where AI credits are bundled and bought up incrementally as they run out.

## When it fits

- Developers who already have an active Claude, GPT, or Gemini subscription/API key and want to avoid paying twice for AI usage
- Teams/solo builders who want an agent that keeps monitoring and auto-fixing an app after initial deployment, not just a one-time code generator
- Agencies managing multiple small client projects needing isolated, persistent per-app agents and custom domains
- Builders who want to ship and iterate on apps from a phone, not just a desktop IDE
- Projects needing fast integration with common SaaS tools (Gmail, Slack, Notion, Stripe, GitHub, etc.) via managed OAuth

## When it doesn't

- Large-scale, high-compute production workloads requiring fine-grained infrastructure control, custom networking, or enterprise compliance guarantees typical of AWS/GCP/Azure.
- Teams without an existing AI model subscription/API key who want AI costs fully bundled into one flat fee.
