## What makes Netskope different

Netskope positions itself as a **converged security and networking platform** rather than a traditional IaaS provider. Unlike AWS, GCP, or Azure—which layer security on top of compute infrastructure—Netskope is purpose-built for protecting cloud-native workloads, SaaS adoption, and AI deployments with integrated visibility and control.

The core differentiator is the **Netskope One Platform**, which merges Security Service Edge (SSE) and Secure Access Service Edge (SASE) into a single cloud-native architecture. This eliminates the need for separate point solutions (VPNs, firewalls, DLP tools, CASB) while providing zero-trust enforcement across users, devices, apps, and data—whether on-premises, in the cloud, or on the internet.

Netskope's **NewEdge Network** is a proprietary global cloud backbone designed for low-latency security processing without backhauling traffic. The platform also emphasizes AI security posture management and GenAI guardrails, addressing the emerging need to secure generative AI adoption without throttling developer velocity.

## Pricing model

Netskope operates on a **subscription-based model** with per-user or per-device licensing, though exact pricing is not publicly listed. The company offers tailored quotes based on organization size, modules selected (SSE, SASE, AI Security, etc.), and region.

A **self-service test drive** is available for Netskope Private Access at no cost, allowing prospective customers to evaluate the platform hands-on before committing. Volume discounts and multi-year commitments are standard for enterprise customers. The lack of transparent per-seat pricing online is typical for enterprise security vendors but contrasts with the pay-as-you-go models of hyperscale compute providers.

## When it fits

- **SaaS-first organizations** needing CASB and SaaS security posture management without managing on-premises security infrastructure
- **Zero-trust architecture migrations** replacing legacy VPNs and perimeter-based access controls
- **Regulated industries** (financial services, healthcare, government) requiring data loss prevention, compliance monitoring, and audit trails
- **AI adoption programs** requiring GenAI security controls, AI gateway policies, and usage visibility
- **Multi-cloud environments** where cloud-agnostic security and networking simplification across AWS, Azure, GCP, and on-premises is a priority

## When it doesn't

Netskope is a **security and access control platform, not a compute provider**—it cannot host applications, databases, or workloads. Organizations looking for Infrastructure-as-a-Service (compute, storage, networking primitives) should use AWS, Azure, GCP, or other IaaS providers; Netskope complements rather than replaces those services.
